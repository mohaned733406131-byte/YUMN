# UC-283 — Arbitrate a Dispute and Choose the Prevailing Party

## Header Info
- **Use Case ID:** UC-283
- **Title:** Arbitrate a Dispute and Choose the Prevailing Party
- **Actor:** Admin (ACT-04)
- **Trigger:** The evidence window for an open dispute has closed (or the facts are sufficient) and a party awaits the platform's final decision
- **Priority:** P0
- **Goal:** End the dispute with an audited buyer/vendor verdict that unfreezes escrow into the correct path — refund or release.
- **Preconditions:** Dispute open, escrow frozen for the affected sub-order (BR-ORD-05); evidence assembled (UC-284, UC-282); Admin is the final arbiter (BR-RET-06); a reason is available for the record.

## Main Scenario
1. Admin opens the dispute detail (`GET /disputes/{id}`, API-RET-015) — ground, notes, evidence list and timeline.
2. Admin reviews the evidence pack, the delivery proof (UC-282) and the order timeline within scope (BR-ORD-09).
3. Admin calls `POST /disputes/{id}/resolve` with `{ outcome: "BUYER"|"VENDOR", reason }` (API-RET-017).
4. System validates state and reason, moves `DISPUTED → REFUNDED` (buyer win) or `DISPUTED → COMPLETED` (vendor win), clears `escrowFrozen` into the chosen path, and writes one audit entry (BR-RET-06).
5. Buyer win: the refund flow executes — escrow first, then vendor payable — with proportional commission reversal (BR-RET-07); vendor win: escrow releases on its normal schedule.

## Alternative Scenarios
- **A1 — Vendor prevails:** sub-order reaches `COMPLETED`, escrow unfreezes toward release, no refund and no commission reversal are started.
- **A2 — Dispute covers one sub-order of a multi-store order:** only that sub-order's escrow was frozen; the other sub-orders and the master keep their own states (BR-ORD-05).
- **A3 — Non-admin attempt:** Moderator, vendor or buyer calling resolve receives `ARBITRATION_FORBIDDEN` — arbitration is Admin-only (API-RET-017).

## Exception Scenarios
- **E1 — Missing reason:** `REASON_REQUIRED` → no transition, dispute stays open, escrow stays frozen (API-RET-017).
- **E2 — Concurrent second resolution:** the dispute was resolved moments earlier → `STATE_CONFLICT`; no double refund, history unchanged.
- **E3 — Refund execution fails after the verdict:** the resolution stands with `refund.status` reported pending; the credit completes later through the idempotent refund engine — no half-written ledger rows.

## Postconditions
- Success: dispute resolved to buyer or vendor, escrow unfrozen into refund or release, sub-order on a legal canonical state, exactly one audit entry naming Admin, reason and outcome (BR-RET-06).
- Failure: dispute still open, escrow still frozen, no refund started, no state change.

## Business Rules Applied
- BR-ORD-05, BR-RET-06, BR-RET-07

## Related Requirements

## Permissions / Data / External Dependencies
- **Permissions:** Admin (ACT-04) final arbiter (API-RET-017); Moderator read-only; buyer/vendor never resolve their own case.
- **Data involved:** dispute record, evidence, sub-order state and `version`, escrow holds, refund/ledger rows, audit entries.
- **External dependencies:** escrow and refund engines (block B07); append-only audit store.

## Acceptance Criteria
- **AC-UC283-01:** Given an open dispute, when Admin resolves it with outcome `BUYER` and a reason, then the sub-order reaches `REFUNDED`, escrow unfreezes, and one audit entry exists (BR-RET-06, BR-RET-07).
- **AC-UC283-02:** Given outcome `VENDOR`, when the resolution completes, then the sub-order reaches `COMPLETED` with no refund started (BR-ORD-05).
- **AC-UC283-03:** Given a non-admin caller, when resolve is attempted, then it is rejected with `ARBITRATION_FORBIDDEN`.
- **AC-UC283-04:** Given a resolve call without a reason, when it is made, then nothing changes and escrow stays frozen (BR-ORD-05).
