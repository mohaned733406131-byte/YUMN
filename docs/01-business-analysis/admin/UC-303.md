# UC-303 — Track Critical Security Findings Against the 7-Day Remediation SLA

## Header Info
- **Use Case ID:** UC-303
- **Title:** Track Critical Security Findings Against the 7-Day Remediation SLA
- **Actor:** Admin (ACT-04)
- **Trigger:** A security finding is confirmed at CRITICAL severity and its remediation clock starts (`09-security/core/security-controls.md` §6, SEC-C-24)
- **Priority:** P1
- **Goal:** Keep every confirmed CRITICAL finding inside the 7-day fix window — with evidence — and surface breaches before the SLA lapses.
- **Preconditions:** Finding registry holds severity, status and confirmed date; SLA tracking starts on confirmation (CRITICAL ≤ 7 days, HIGH ≤ 30 days); closure requires fix or accepted-risk decision plus evidence.

## Main Scenario
1. Admin opens the findings registry filtered to open CRITICAL items and sorts by confirmation date.
2. For each, Admin computes days elapsed against the 7-day bound and notes remediation evidence status (test/audit link).
3. Admin chases the owning team for findings inside the window, verifying progress against the linked control.
4. Admin records status changes on the registry entry — a finding is never silently deleted; closure carries fix/accepted-risk, evidence, severity re-check.
5. Findings that cannot make the window are escalated before the deadline, not after.

## Alternative Scenarios
- **A1 — HIGH findings in view:** tracked against the 30-day bound on the same board — same workflow, different clock.
- **A2 — Accepted risk instead of a fix:** closure records the decision, evidence and severity re-check — the SLA outcome is "decided within window", not "silently closed".
- **A3 — Finding re-opened by regression:** severity re-checked, a new confirmation date starts the clock, history preserved.

## Exception Scenarios
- **E1 — SLA breach:** the deadline passes without fix or decision → flagged as a control breach with escalation; the entry stays open and visible, never suppressed.
- **E2 — Severity reclassified during the window:** the clock is re-evaluated against the new severity with the change documented — not used to dodge the original deadline.
- **E3 — Registry unavailable:** tracking is reported as incomplete for the period; a blank board is never treated as a clean board.

## Postconditions
- Success: every CRITICAL finding has a live status, evidence path and, at closure, a complete decision record; the registry stays append-honest.
- Failure: nothing is removed or downgraded to look compliant — open findings remain open and visible.

## Business Rules Applied
- No DOC-BA-005 rule governs remediation SLAs — rule of record is SEC-REQ-012 (critical vulns fixed ≤ 7 days, `09-security/core/security-controls.md` §6)

## Related Requirements
- FR-020 (Platform Administration, Settings & Audit)

## Permissions / Data / External Dependencies
- **Permissions:** Admin (ACT-04) findings registry review; security owner process hands off to Super Admin for escalation.
- **Data involved:** security findings registry (severity, status, dates, evidence links), control catalog (read).
- **External dependencies:** CI/CD scan output feeding the registry (`09-security/core/security-controls.md`, SEC-C-23); test/audit evidence store.

## Acceptance Criteria
- **AC-UC303-01:** Given a CRITICAL finding confirmed today, when 7 days elapse without fix or decision, then it is flagged as an SLA breach and escalated.
- **AC-UC303-02:** Given a closed finding, when its record is inspected, then fix/accepted-risk, evidence link and severity re-check are all present.
- **AC-UC303-03:** Given an open CRITICAL finding, when the registry is read, then it appears as open with its confirmation date — never silently removed.
- **AC-UC303-04:** Given a HIGH finding, when tracked, then its clock runs against the 30-day bound on the same board.
