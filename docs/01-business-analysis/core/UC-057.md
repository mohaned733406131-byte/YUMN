# UC-057 — Reverse Commission Proportionally on Refund

## Header Info
- **Use Case ID:** UC-057
- **Title:** Reverse Commission Proportionally on Refund
- **Actor:** System (ACT-07) — refund adjustment leg (no interactive login)
- **Trigger:** A refund is confirmed for an order whose commission was already taken at escrow release (FR-016)
- **Priority:** P1
- **Goal:** Reverse exactly the proportional share of commission that the refunded amount represents, so neither platform nor vendor absorbs the other's share of a refund.
- **Preconditions:** Commission was computed at release (BR-ESC-04); the refund amount and original commission are known from the refund chain (BR-RET-07).

## Main Scenario
1. The refund is confirmed for a sub-order (return or cancellation path, FR-016).
2. The refund draws first from escrow-held funds; if escrow is insufficient the remainder draws from vendor payable (BR-ESC-07).
3. The system computes the reversed commission share proportional to the refunded amount against the original commission (BR-ESC-04).
4. The reversal is posted as balanced ledger entries inside an ACID transaction — payable debited back, platform commission credited (FR-014).
5. The refund chain records the chain state: refund → proportional commission reversal → escrow adjustment, in order (BR-RET-07).
6. The adjusted payable and commission figures reflect the reversal in the vendor's finance view (FR-014).

## Alternative Scenarios
- **A1 — Full-order refund:** the entire commission is reversed — proportional share equals the whole amount (BR-ESC-04).
- **A2 — Partial refund:** only the refunded fraction's commission reverses; the retained portion keeps its commission (BR-ESC-04).
- **A3 — Refund before release:** commission not yet taken → no reversal needed; escrow simply never releases for the refunded portion (BR-ESC-07).

## Exception Scenarios
- **E1 — Escrow insufficient:** the shortfall draws from vendor payable (vendor liability) — the refund total is still fully covered (BR-ESC-07).
- **E2 — Adjustment job fails:** transaction rolls back → refund and commission stay consistent; the job retries (FR-016).
- **E3 — Concurrent payout execution:** row-level lock serializes reversal against payout debit — commission reversal is never lost to an in-flight payout (FR-014).

## Postconditions
- Success: proportional commission reversed, ledger balanced, refund chain complete, payable and finance views updated.
- Failure: no commission or payable change; the refund flow retries; no partial reversal persists.

## Business Rules Applied
- BR-ESC-04, BR-ESC-07, BR-RET-07

## Related Requirements
- FR-014 (Escrow, Commission & Vendor Payouts), FR-016 (Returns & Refunds)

## Permissions / Data / External Dependencies
- **Permissions:** service identity only (ACT-07); no human edits commission records — reversals are system-posted entries.
- **Data involved:** commission records, escrow holdings, vendor payables, ledger entries, refund chain records.
- **External dependencies:** wallet/refund engine, money ledger service, finance read models.

## Acceptance Criteria
- **AC-UC057-01:** Given 100 YER commission on a full refund, when the refund confirms, then 100 YER commission is reversed (BR-ESC-04).
- **AC-UC057-02:** Given a refund covering half the order value, when the reversal runs, then half the commission reverses (BR-ESC-04).
- **AC-UC057-03:** Given escrow holding less than the refund, when the refund draws, then the shortfall debits vendor payable (BR-ESC-07).
- **AC-UC057-04:** Given a reversal, when it posts, then the chain order refund → commission reversal → escrow adjustment holds (BR-RET-07).
