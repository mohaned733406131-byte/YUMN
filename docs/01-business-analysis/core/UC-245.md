# UC-245 — Throttle Per-Channel Notification Sends to Protect Provider Quotas

## Header Info
- **Use Case ID:** UC-245
- **Title:** Throttle Per-Channel Notification Sends to Protect Provider Quotas
- **Actor:** System (ACT-07) — notification delivery rate limiter (no interactive login)
- **Trigger:** Per-channel send volume approaches the configured provider rate limit
- **Priority:** P1
- **Goal:** Keep every channel inside its quota by slowing the lane down instead of burning the provider allowance.
- **Preconditions:** Per-channel delivery lanes exist (channel = job lane of `b10.notification.delivery`); the queue limiter is configured; provider quotas are known.

## Main Scenario
1. Delivery jobs accumulate in the per-channel lanes (sms | whatsapp | push).
2. The queue limiter admits jobs at the configured per-channel rate — excess jobs wait in the queue instead of firing (backpressure).
3. Throughput stays within the provider quota; lanes throttle independently, so an SMS backlog does not stall push.
4. Ordering-sensitive flows such as OTP keep per-recipient sequencing while queued.
5. Queue depth and wait time are exported as metrics; sustained depth signals a quota or provider problem (NFR-014).
6. When volume falls, the backlog drains at the same governed rate — no unthrottled burst catch-up.

## Alternative Scenarios
- **A1 — One provider lane degraded:** that lane throttles further and retries; the other channels are unaffected.
- **A2 — Burst below the quota:** the limiter admits at full configured rate; no user-visible delay.
- **A3 — Security notices during throttle:** they queue behind the same limiter — throttling delays them, never drops them.

## Exception Scenarios
- **E1 — Limiter configured too low:** backlog grows continuously → queue-depth alerting raises an operations signal (NFR-014).
- **E2 — Provider rejects over-quota sends anyway:** failures retry with backoff and dead-letter with an alert; recipients get them on retry or failover (BR-PLT-02).
- **E3 — Retry storm while throttled:** retries consume the same governed rate — they cannot bypass the limiter and re-breach the quota.

## Postconditions
- Success: send rate per channel stays at or below the configured quota; backlog is bounded and draining; nothing is dropped.
- Failure: jobs wait or dead-letter with an alert — no quota-breach wave, no silent message loss (NFR-007).

## Business Rules Applied
- BR-PLT-02 — generic job retry/DLQ discipline only; no BR in DOC-BA-005 governs per-channel throttling (it is pipeline backpressure per FR-017).

## Related Requirements
- FR-017 (Notifications & Messaging)

## Permissions / Data / External Dependencies
- **Permissions:** service identity only (ACT-07) — configures and enforces its own limiter; no human actor bypasses it.
- **Data involved:** queue lanes, limiter configuration, queued jobs, queue-depth metrics.
- **External dependencies:** BullMQ queue limiter; SMS and WhatsApp providers (quotas); Prometheus metrics.

## Acceptance Criteria
- **AC-UC245-01:** Given a sustained burst above the quota, when the limiter runs, then admitted sends stay within the configured per-channel rate (FR-017).
- **AC-UC245-02:** Given an SMS backlog, when push jobs arrive, then the push lane continues at its own configured rate.
- **AC-UC245-03:** Given queued jobs when volume falls, when the backlog drains, then every queued message is eventually sent — none dropped.
- **AC-UC245-04:** Given provider rejections, when retries exhaust, then jobs dead-letter with an alert (BR-PLT-02).
