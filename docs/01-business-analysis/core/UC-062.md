# UC-062 — Retry Failed Jobs and Alert on Dead-Letter Depth

## Header Info
- **Use Case ID:** UC-062
- **Title:** Retry Failed Jobs and Alert on Dead-Letter Depth
- **Actor:** System (ACT-07) — job reliability supervisor (no interactive login)
- **Trigger:** A queue job fails on any BullMQ queue (FR-020)
- **Priority:** P1
- **Goal:** Recover transient job failures through bounded retries and surface unrecoverable ones via dead-letter alerts before they become silent data loss.
- **Preconditions:** All queues follow the `{block}.{entity}.{action}` naming and retry policy; queue depth and DLQ depth are exported for monitoring (FR-020).

## Main Scenario
1. A job handler throws → the queue records the failure and schedules a retry with exponential backoff (FR-020).
2. Up to 3 attempts run with jittered backoff; a later attempt may succeed and complete the job normally (FR-020).
3. All 3 attempts exhausted → the job moves to `{queue}.dlq` (FR-020).
4. DLQ depth greater than zero triggers an alert through the monitoring/alerting path (FR-020).
5. Queue depth, wait time and failure rate metrics are exported for dashboards (FR-020).
6. An operator replays the dead-lettered job through admin tooling; the replay is audited (FR-020).

## Alternative Scenarios
- **A1 — Retry succeeds:** the job completes on attempt 2 → no dead-letter, no alert; retries are counted as a reliability signal (FR-020).
- **A2 — Non-retryable failure (validation):** classified permanent-failure → straight to dead-letter without burning retries, still alerted (FR-020).
- **A3 — Stalled job (worker crash):** stalled-job detection re-enqueues it; the stall counts toward reliability metrics, not DLQ (FR-020).

## Exception Scenarios
- **E1 — Alert delivery fails:** the underlying DLQ state persists in monitoring data; alerting retries; nothing is cleared by a missed notification (FR-020).
- **E2 — Redis/queue outage:** workers finish in-flight jobs on shutdown; jobs survive the outage and process after recovery — no job loss (FR-020).
- **E3 — Poison message (repeatedly failing handler):** attempts exhaust predictably → dead-letter + alert; the consumer's error handling keeps the worker alive for other jobs (FR-020).

## Postconditions
- Success: transient failures recovered by retry; unrecoverable jobs sit in DLQ with an active alert; metrics exported.
- Failure: job neither completed nor alerted (alerting outage) — DLQ state still recorded and redisplays on recovery.

## Business Rules Applied
- BR-PLT-01, BR-PLT-02

## Related Requirements
- FR-020 (Platform Administration, Settings & Audit)

## Permissions / Data / External Dependencies
- **Permissions:** service identity operates queues; replay is an admin-console action performed by an operator (FR-020), audited.
- **Data involved:** job records, retry/DLQ counters, alert records, reliability metrics, audit entries.
- **External dependencies:** BullMQ/Redis (C-20), monitoring/alerting stack.

## Acceptance Criteria
- **AC-UC062-01:** Given a job failing once then succeeding, when processed, then it completes without dead-lettering (BR-PLT-02).
- **AC-UC062-02:** Given a job failing all 3 attempts, when exhausted, then it lands in `{queue}.dlq` (BR-PLT-02).
- **AC-UC062-03:** Given DLQ depth greater than zero, when detected, then an alert is raised (BR-PLT-02).
- **AC-UC062-04:** Given any queue, when inspected, then its name matches `{block}.{entity}.{action}` (BR-PLT-01).
