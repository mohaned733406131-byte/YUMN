# UC-216 — Anonymize Profile PII for Accounts Dormant 24 Months After Notice

## Header Info
- **Use Case ID:** UC-216
- **Title:** Anonymize Profile PII for Accounts Dormant 24 Months After Notice
- **Actor:** System (ACT-07) — retention job (no interactive login)
- **Trigger:** The dormancy sweep finds an account with 24 consecutive months without login or user-initiated action
- **Priority:** P2
- **Goal:** Irreversibly anonymize profile PII of long-dormant accounts after a 30-day notice, while financial records keep their referent.
- **Preconditions:** Dormancy clock elapsed (24 months); notice channel available (BR-NTF-01); financial retention floor ≥ 5 years (NFR-019) untouched by this job.

## Main Scenario
1. The dormancy job scans accounts and marks those with 24 consecutive inactive months as dormant.
2. A 30-day notice is attempted over the available notification channels — SMS/WhatsApp (BR-NTF-01).
3. No reactivation inside the notice window → profile PII (name, phone, email) is anonymized irreversibly; the row itself is kept so financial records retain their referent.
4. Address book rows, device tokens and sessions are removed in the same operation (cascade).
5. Reactivation before anonymization restores the account untouched; after it, the identity cannot be restored by design.
6. The dormancy, notice and anonymization evidence is recorded for audit.

## Alternative Scenarios
- **A1 — Activity within 24 months:** the account is skipped and the dormancy clock restarts.
- **A2 — Reactivation during the notice window:** the dormant mark is cleared and the account is restored untouched.
- **A3 — Financial history:** orders and ledger rows are never anonymized by this job — amounts and the de-identified referent survive (NFR-019).

## Exception Scenarios
- **E1 — Notice undeliverable:** the per-channel attempt is recorded; the schedule proceeds — notice is attempted, not guaranteed delivered (BR-NTF-01).
- **E2 — Anonymization fails partway:** the per-account operation is transactional — either the full cascade completes or nothing changes; no half-anonymized profile.
- **E3 — Protected data in scope:** financial and audit rows are never touched by this job; they remain under their own retention floors (NFR-019).

## Postconditions
- Success: profile PII is irreversibly gone; the account row and financial referents survive; sessions and addresses are removed.
- Failure: nothing changes — the account keeps its PII and remains restorable.

## Business Rules Applied
- BR-NTF-01

## Related Requirements
- FR-003 (User & Profile Management)

## Permissions / Data / External Dependencies
- **Permissions:** service identity only (ACT-07) — retention path; no interactive role can trigger or reverse anonymization.
- **Data involved:** profile PII columns, addresses, device tokens, sessions, dormancy evidence; financial rows read-only (untouched).
- **External dependencies:** notification pipeline for the 30-day notice (BR-NTF-01 channels).

## Acceptance Criteria
- **AC-UC216-01:** Given 24 months of inactivity and an unheeded 30-day notice, when the job runs, then profile PII is anonymized irreversibly (FR-003).
- **AC-UC216-02:** Given an account reactivated inside the notice window, when the job runs next, then no PII is altered.
- **AC-UC216-03:** Given an anonymized account, when its financial history is read, then amounts and the referent remain intact (NFR-019).
