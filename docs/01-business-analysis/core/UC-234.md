# UC-234 — Credit Refunds Into Frozen Wallets While Debits Stay Blocked

## Header Info
- **Use Case ID:** UC-234
- **Title:** Credit Refunds Into Frozen Wallets While Debits Stay Blocked
- **Actor:** System (ACT-07) — refund/freeze handler (no interactive login)
- **Trigger:** A refund is due to a customer whose wallet is frozen for legal or security reasons
- **Priority:** P1
- **Goal:** Keep the freeze absolute for spending while money owed to the customer still arrives.
- **Preconditions:** Wallet frozen by admin with a recorded reason (BR-PAY-09); refund approved; refunds always credit the wallet; ledger append-only (BR-PAY-06).

## Main Scenario
1. A refund is approved for an order paid from the frozen wallet.
2. System evaluates the freeze rule: frozen wallets cannot pay or top up, but receive refunds (BR-PAY-09).
3. The refund credit is posted to the wallet — balance increases even though the wallet is frozen (BR-PAY-09).
4. Balanced double-entry rows record the refund (BR-PAY-06); the freeze flag stays set.
5. Any debit attempt (order payment or top-up) on the frozen wallet is rejected with a freeze error while the credit succeeds.
6. The freeze and its reason remain in the audit trail; only admin action unfreezes.

## Alternative Scenarios
- **A1 — Unfrozen wallet:** the credit behaves normally; only the freeze flag differs.
- **A2 — Top-up attempt while frozen:** rejected — freezes block funding as well as spending (BR-PAY-09).
- **A3 — Partial refund:** the credit follows the refund amount rules; the freeze never alters refund totals.

## Exception Scenarios
- **E1 — Refund posting fails:** the refund state and credit stay consistent — retry until credited (BR-PLT-02); a frozen-wallet refund is never silently dropped.
- **E2 — Debit racing the freeze:** the freeze flag is read under the same row lock as the balance check — a debit either sees frozen (rejected) or completed before the freeze, never both.
- **E3 — Attempt to edit a balance instead of posting:** impossible — postings are append-only; freezes change flags, never balances (BR-PAY-06).

## Postconditions
- Success: the refund is credited to the frozen wallet with balanced rows; the freeze remains intact.
- Failure: no debit or top-up occurred; the balance is never negative.

## Business Rules Applied
- BR-PAY-09

## Related Requirements
- FR-013 (Wallet & Payment Processing)

## Permissions / Data / External Dependencies
- **Permissions:** service identity only (ACT-07) — posts refunds; freeze/unfreeze is admin-only; no role can edit balances.
- **Data involved:** wallet freeze flag and reason, refund records, ledger entries, audit entry.
- **External dependencies:** refund flow (upstream); PostgreSQL row lock for flag+balance consistency.

## Acceptance Criteria
- **AC-UC234-01:** Given a frozen wallet, when a refund is processed, then the balance increases (BR-PAY-09).
- **AC-UC234-02:** Given a frozen wallet, when an order payment or top-up is attempted, then it is rejected (BR-PAY-09).
- **AC-UC234-03:** Given the refund credit, when it commits, then balanced ledger rows exist (BR-PAY-06).
