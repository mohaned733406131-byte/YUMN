# UC-237 — Route VAT Rounding Residuals to the Platform Rounding Account

## Header Info
- **Use Case ID:** UC-237
- **Title:** Route VAT Rounding Residuals to the Platform Rounding Account
- **Actor:** System (ACT-07) — pricing/validation service (no interactive login)
- **Trigger:** Checkout computes VAT and a fractional residual appears when rounding to whole YER
- **Priority:** P2
- **Goal:** Round half-up per sub-order and post every residual to the platform rounding account — no silent drift in the ledger.
- **Preconditions:** VAT = 15% × (subtotal − discount), shipping untaxed (BR-FIN-01); half-up rounding to whole YER per sub-order (BR-FIN-05); integer YER amounts end-to-end.

## Main Scenario
1. Checkout computes VAT = 15% × (cart subtotal − coupon discount); shipping is not taxed (BR-FIN-01).
2. The sub-order total is rounded half-up to whole YER at the sub-order level (BR-FIN-05).
3. Where the VAT arithmetic produces a fractional residual, the difference is posted to the platform rounding account rather than absorbed silently (BR-FIN-05).
4. The rounded integer-YER total is what the wallet is charged.
5. The breakdown shown to the buyer matches exactly what is charged, VAT line included (BR-FIN-01).
6. Daily reconciliation verifies balanced entries with the rounding account included.

## Alternative Scenarios
- **A1 — Whole-YER VAT:** no residual arises and nothing is posted to the rounding account.
- **A2 — Free-shipping coupon:** shipping remains untaxed; rounding applies to the taxed base only (BR-FIN-01).
- **A3 — Discount reduces the VAT base:** VAT is computed on subtotal minus discount before rounding (BR-FIN-01).

## Exception Scenarios
- **E1 — Residual not posted:** reconciliation would detect the imbalance — balanced pairs are mandatory, so the posting is required (BR-PAY-06).
- **E2 — Float arithmetic attempted:** amounts are integer YER end-to-end; fractional intermediates are handled by explicit half-up rounding, never floats.
- **E3 — Replay of a rounded order:** idempotency returns the original rounded total; the residual is posted once (BR-PLT-03).

## Postconditions
- Success: the charged total is integer YER, the residual sits in the platform rounding account, and the ledger balances.
- Failure: no charge occurs without a matching balanced posting; no rounding drift accumulates.

## Business Rules Applied
- BR-FIN-01, BR-FIN-05

## Related Requirements
- FR-013 (Wallet & Payment Processing)

## Permissions / Data / External Dependencies
- **Permissions:** service identity only (ACT-07) — pricing computation at checkout; no human edits rounding postings.
- **Data involved:** VAT computation inputs, sub-order totals, ledger entries (rounding account), order breakdowns.
- **External dependencies:** pricing domain service; double-entry ledger with the rounding account.

## Acceptance Criteria
- **AC-UC237-01:** Given a VAT amount with a fractional part, when the sub-order total is computed, then it is rounded half-up to whole YER (BR-FIN-05).
- **AC-UC237-02:** Given a rounding residual, when the transaction posts, then the difference appears in the platform rounding account (BR-FIN-05).
- **AC-UC237-03:** Given a cart subtotal of 100,000 YER with no discount, when VAT is computed, then it is 15,000 YER with shipping untaxed (BR-FIN-01).
