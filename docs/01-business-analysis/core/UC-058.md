# UC-058 — Execute Wallet Refunds to the Customer

## Header Info
- **Use Case ID:** UC-058
- **Title:** Execute Wallet Refunds to the Customer
- **Actor:** System (ACT-07) — refund credit job (no interactive login)
- **Trigger:** A return reaches `REFUNDED` or an order cancellation completes, queuing `b09.return.refund-credit` (FR-016)
- **Priority:** P0
- **Goal:** Credit the customer's wallet for a confirmed refund within 3 business days, exactly once, with balanced ledger postings — wallet only, never external cash-out.
- **Preconditions:** Refund amount determined (item value, shipping only per fault, FR-016); a wallet exists for the customer (FR-013); refund source funds already resolved (escrow/payable draw runs in UC-057).

## Main Scenario
1. The refund engine enqueues a credit job on `b09.return.refund-credit` carrying the refund reference (FR-016).
2. The consumer re-reads the refund state fresh — payloads carry IDs only, never amounts (FR-016).
3. If not already credited, the wallet is credited inside one ACID transaction with balanced double-entry ledger rows (BR-PLT-04).
4. The credit is strictly wallet-bound — no card/bank cash-out path exists (BR-PAY-07).
5. The refund moves to credited state; the ledger credit reference is recorded against the refund (FR-013).
6. The customer receives a notification and can see the credit in the wallet statement (FR-016, FR-013).

## Alternative Scenarios
- **A1 — Frozen customer wallet:** the credit still posts — frozen wallets cannot pay but always receive refunds (BR-PAY-07).
- **A2 — Credit near the 3-day bound:** the job sweeper ensures the credit lands within 3 business days of `REFUNDED` (BR-RET-04).
- **A3 — Multi-item return:** one aggregated credit per refund reference, with per-item composition retained for the receipt (FR-016).

## Exception Scenarios
- **E1 — Job fails:** retried with backoff then dead-letter with alert; the refund stays pending-credit and the customer is kept within the 3-business-day commitment by the next sweep (BR-RET-04).
- **E2 — Duplicate enqueue (state change + sweep):** idempotent credit keyed by refund reference → second run no-ops; balance credited once (FR-013).
- **E3 — Ledger write failure:** the whole credit transaction rolls back — no half-credited wallet; retry re-runs the complete posting (BR-PLT-04).

## Postconditions
- Success: wallet balance increased once by the refund amount, balanced ledger rows appended, refund credited, customer notified.
- Failure: no balance or ledger change; refund remains pending credit; job retries.

## Business Rules Applied
- BR-PAY-07, BR-RET-04, BR-PLT-04

## Related Requirements
- FR-013 (Wallet & Payment Processing), FR-016 (Returns & Refunds)

## Permissions / Data / External Dependencies
- **Permissions:** service identity only (ACT-07); refunds credit the customer's own wallet — the customer never specifies a destination (BR-PAY-07).
- **Data involved:** refund records, wallet balances, ledger entries, notifications.
- **External dependencies:** BullMQ/Redis queue `b09.return.refund-credit` (C-20), ledger service, notification fan-out (B10).

## Acceptance Criteria
- **AC-UC058-01:** Given a `REFUNDED` return, when the credit job runs within 3 business days, then the wallet balance rises by the refund amount once (BR-RET-04).
- **AC-UC058-02:** Given the same refund enqueued twice, when both run, then only one credit posts (FR-013).
- **AC-UC058-03:** Given a refunded customer with a frozen wallet, when credit runs, then the credit still posts (BR-PAY-07).
- **AC-UC058-04:** Given a successful credit, when inspected, then balanced debit/credit ledger rows exist inside one ACID transaction (BR-PLT-04).
