# UC-071 — Deliver Follower Marketing Notifications on New Products

## Header Info
- **Use Case ID:** UC-071
- **Title:** Deliver Follower Marketing Notifications on New Products
- **Actor:** System (ACT-07) — marketing fan-out (no interactive login)
- **Trigger:** Vendor publishes a new product or an offer in a store that has followers
- **Priority:** P2
- **Goal:** Notify each follower of a store's new products on the channels they still accept, without ever violating per-category opt-outs.
- **Preconditions:** Store has followers (BR-VND-05); followers' notification preferences recorded per category and channel (BR-NTF-05); fan-out queue running.

## Main Scenario
1. A new-product/offer event is emitted and enqueued on the notification fan-out queue (`b10.notification.fanout`).
2. The worker resolves the store's follower list (BR-VND-05) and expands it into per-recipient delivery intents.
3. Each intent is filtered against the recipient's marketing preferences: a channel opted out for the marketing category is dropped (BR-NTF-05).
4. Surviving intents are rendered in the recipient's locale and handed to their channels (in-app, push, SMS/WhatsApp as configured).
5. Delivery outcomes are recorded so vendors can see reach and skipped recipients are auditable.

## Alternative Scenarios
- **A1 — Follower opted out of marketing on every channel:** All intents suppressed (BR-NTF-05); the follower still sees new products by browsing — no message is sent.
- **A2 — Follower opted out of some channels:** Only opted-in channels receive the message; others are skipped per preference.
- **A3 — Store with no followers:** The event completes as a no-op with zero intents — no queue backlog.

## Exception Scenarios
- **E1 — Channel provider failure:** The affected intents retry through the delivery lanes; other channels for the same recipient still proceed.
- **E2 — Fan-out job crash:** The job retries and, on exhaustion, dead-letters with an alert; recipient-level dedup prevents duplicate blasts on replay.
- **E3 — Preference updated mid-flight:** Delivery reads preferences at send time; a just-made opt-out wins — the message is suppressed rather than queued late.

## Postconditions
- Opted-in followers are notified on their enabled channels; opted-out ones receive nothing (BR-NTF-05).
- Store follower semantics unchanged (BR-VND-05) — following/unfollowing is untouched by delivery.
- Every intent has a terminal outcome (sent or suppressed with reason).

## Business Rules Applied
- BR-VND-05, BR-NTF-05

## Related Requirements
- FR-017 (Notifications & Messaging), FR-008 (Store Management & Storefront Configuration)
- Cross-reference (spec relation): fan-out delivers the eligibility set UC-008 step4 establishes when a customer follows a store.

## Permissions / Data / External Dependencies
- **Permissions:** Service identity (System, ACT-07); followers manage their own preferences (notification settings screens).
- **Data involved:** Follow edges, preference rows, notification intents, rendered messages, delivery receipts.
- **External dependencies:** BullMQ notification fan-out; channel providers (SMS/WhatsApp/push); in-app inbox.

## Acceptance Criteria
- **AC-UC071-01:** Given a follower with marketing enabled on push, when a store publishes a product, then that follower receives one push.
- **AC-UC071-02:** Given a follower who opted out of marketing on all channels, when the fan-out runs, then no message is delivered (BR-NTF-05).
- **AC-UC071-03:** Given a store with no followers, when a product is published, then no delivery intents are created.
- **AC-UC071-04:** Given a retried fan-out job, when it replays, then no follower receives duplicates.
