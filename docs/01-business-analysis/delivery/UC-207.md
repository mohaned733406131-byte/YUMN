# UC-207 — Toggle Availability Online/Offline

## Header Info
- **Use Case ID:** UC-207
- **Title:** Toggle Availability Online/Offline
- **Actor:** Delivery Provider (ACT-03)
- **Trigger:** Courier flips the availability switch in the courier app (Queue or Profile tab, S5)
- **Priority:** P1
- **Goal:** Let the courier stop or start receiving delivery offers without losing or gaining any assignment implicitly.
- **Preconditions:** Courier authenticated; going offline requires all active jobs resolved first (completed or released, UC-205).

## Main Scenario
1. Courier flips the switch → client `PUT /courier/availability` `{ availability: "ONLINE" | "OFFLINE" }` (API-SHP-016).
2. System persists the new availability with a `changedAt` timestamp and echoes it in the response.
3. Going ONLINE: the courier becomes eligible again for zone offer broadcast — the available-offers list (UC-025 step 1) starts returning jobs for their zones (BR-SHP-04).
4. Going OFFLINE: the offer fan-out skips the courier and accept attempts are refused (`COURIER_OFFLINE`); already-assigned jobs stay assigned and remain visible in "my deliveries".
5. The current availability is always readable back on the profile screen (API-SHP-014).

## Alternative Scenarios
- **A1 — Already in the target state:** repeated toggle is an idempotent no-op returning the current availability and its original `changedAt`.
- **A2 — Offline after the last job completes:** no active jobs remain → the toggle succeeds immediately; history and earnings are untouched.
- **A3 — Offer list cached while offline:** a stale cached offer may still render, but accepting it is refused (`COURIER_OFFLINE`) and the offer simply goes to another eligible courier (BR-SHP-04).

## Exception Scenarios
- **E1 — Active jobs outstanding:** going offline is rejected with `STATE_CONFLICT`; availability stays `ONLINE` until jobs are completed or released (UC-205).
- **E2 — Network failure on toggle:** no local state change; the switch reverts to the last server-confirmed value on refresh.
- **E3 — Toggle races an incoming offer accept:** the server-side check decides — an offline courier's accept is refused, a freshly online courier competes under first-accept-wins (BR-SHP-04).

## Postconditions
- Success: availability equals the chosen value; `OFFLINE` → no new offers; `ONLINE` → zone offers resume (BR-SHP-04).
- Failure: availability unchanged; no assignment, state, or money change.

## Business Rules Applied
- BR-SHP-04

## Related Requirements
- FR-015 (Shipping & Delivery)
- **Related:** complements UC-025 step 1 (offers list) — offline couriers receive no entries in that list and cannot accept.

## Permissions / Data / External Dependencies
- **Permissions:** courier toggles only their own availability; admins can disable a courier's zone eligibility separately (DOC-OVR-007).
- **Data involved:** availability flag + `changedAt` on the courier profile; offer rows are unaffected.
- **External dependencies:** offer broadcast queue `b08.shipping.assign-offer` (`06-backend/background-processing.md` §1); offer feed `GET /deliveries/available` (UC-025).

## Acceptance Criteria
- **AC-UC207-01:** Given an ONLINE courier, when toggled OFFLINE, then the offers list excludes them and accept attempts return `COURIER_OFFLINE`.
- **AC-UC207-02:** Given an OFFLINE courier, when toggled ONLINE, then zone offers resume for them (BR-SHP-04).
- **AC-UC207-03:** Given active jobs, when the courier goes offline, then the request conflicts and availability stays `ONLINE`.
- **AC-UC207-04:** Given a successful toggle, when the profile is re-read, then availability and `changedAt` reflect the change.
