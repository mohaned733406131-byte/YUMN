# UC-173 — Review the Dispute Resolution Outcome

## Header Info
- **Use Case ID:** UC-173
- **Title:** Review the Dispute Resolution Outcome
- **Actor:** Customer (ACT-01)
- **Trigger:** Customer opens their dispute list or a decided dispute
- **Priority:** P1
- **Goal:** Show the customer the admin's final dispute decision, its reason, and the resulting refund status on their wallet.
- **Preconditions:** Authenticated; dispute involves the customer's own order; a decision may or may not exist yet (A3).

## Main Scenario
1. Customer opens Disputes → `GET /disputes` (API-RET-014), own-scoped feed with state, `escrowFrozen` and `dueAt`.
2. Opens one dispute → `GET /disputes/{id}` (API-RET-015): ground, notes, both parties' evidence, timeline and `resolution`.
3. On `RESOLVED_BUYER`: escrow unfreezes into the refund path; the paid amount (integer YER) is credited to the customer's wallet — never external cash-out (BR-PAY-07); the credit executes through the refund flow (cross-reference: UC-058).
4. On `RESOLVED_VENDOR`: the order continues to completion; no refund is created.
5. The admin decision is final and recorded with an audit entry (BR-RET-06).

## Alternative Scenarios
- **A1 — Buyer win, refund pending:** the refund block shows status and expected credit; the customer can follow the refund receipt (conceptual `GET /orders/{id}/refund`).
- **A2 — Vendor win:** the customer reads the `resolution` reason; escrow releases normally afterwards.
- **A3 — Dispute still `OPEN`:** no resolution block — evidence status and due date shown instead.

## Exception Scenarios
- **E1 — Foreign or unknown dispute:** `NOT_FOUND` (owner scope).
- **E2 — Wallet credit not yet visible:** refund status stays `PENDING`/`COMPOSED` until the credit posts; customer re-fetches, ledger unchanged.
- **E3 — Invalid or expired session:** `AUTH_INVALID`; no outcome rendered.

## Postconditions
- Success: customer sees the final decision and, on buyer win, the wallet credit reference in integer YER; escrow unfrozen.
- Failure: no data rendered; dispute state untouched by this read.

## Business Rules Applied
- BR-RET-06, BR-PAY-07

## Related Requirements
- FR-016 (Returns & Refunds — dispute resolution)
- **Relation:** UC-058 executes the wallet refund credit after a buyer win; UC-171/UC-172 produced the case decided here.

## Permissions / Data / External Dependencies
- **Permissions:** parties read their own dispute; admin alone resolves (BR-RET-06); customer receives refund only to own wallet (BR-PAY-07).
- **Data involved:** dispute resolution, timeline, escrow state, refund status, wallet ledger credit.
- **External dependencies:** returns/disputes service (B09); wallet/ledger service (B07); audit log (B13).

## Acceptance Criteria
- **AC-UC173-01:** Given `RESOLVED_BUYER`, when detail loads, then outcome, reason and audit-backed resolution are shown (BR-RET-06).
- **AC-UC173-02:** Given a buyer-win refund, when it executes, then the wallet is credited in integer YER with no external payout (BR-PAY-07).
- **AC-UC173-03:** Given `RESOLVED_VENDOR`, when detail loads, then no refund exists for that sub-order (BR-PAY-07).
- **AC-UC173-04:** Given another customer's dispute, when requested, then `NOT_FOUND`.
