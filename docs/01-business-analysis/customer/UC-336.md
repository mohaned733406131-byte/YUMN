# UC-336 — Receive the 24-Hour Escalation Notice on a Stalled Order

## Header Info
- **Use Case ID:** UC-336
- **Title:** Receive the 24-Hour Escalation Notice on a Stalled Order
- **Actor:** Customer (ACT-01)
- **Trigger:** The customer's order is still at `CONFIRMED` 24 hours after confirmation
- **Priority:** P2
- **Goal:** Tell the customer the order was escalated to admin review — never silently auto-cancelled.
- **Preconditions:** Sub-order reached `CONFIRMED` (FR-012); notification preferences readable (FR-017); escalation job runs (EC-15).

## Main Scenario
1. System detects the sub-order still `CONFIRMED` 24 h after confirmation (edge-cases EC-15).
2. The order is escalated to admin review and flagged `SLA_24H` (orders.md behavior notes).
3. The customer receives the escalation notification — in-app always, plus opted-in channels (BR-ORD-10, FR-017).
4. The order timeline gains an append-only escalation entry with actor and timestamp (BR-ORD-03).
5. The customer opens the notice → deep-links to the order, sees the stall explained and a support path; the order is not cancelled (BR-ORD-10).

## Alternative Scenarios
- **A1 — Vendor confirms before 24 h:** no escalation; the flow ends.
- **A2 — Vendor acts after escalation:** normal state progression continues and the stall notice clears on the next state change.
- **A3 — Marketing opted out:** the order-category notice is still delivered — opt-out never suppresses it (BR-NTF-05).

## Exception Scenarios
- **E1 — Channel delivery fails:** the in-app record is guaranteed; queue retries follow 3× backoff then DLQ (BR-PLT-02).
- **E2 — Customer reads later:** the notice persists in the inbox with its deep link (FR-017).
- **E3 — State changed before the tap:** the deep link re-fetches the entity — a notification never embeds authoritative state.

## Postconditions
- Success: escalation notice exists in-app and in the timeline; the order stays at `CONFIRMED` — no cancellation.
- Failure: no state change and no refund originates from the notice itself.

## Business Rules Applied
- BR-ORD-10, BR-ORD-03

## Related Requirements
- FR-012 (Order Lifecycle Management — 24 h escalation), FR-017 (Notifications & Messaging — fan-out)
- **Relation:** UC-012 tracks the stalled order; UC-355 is the inbox where the notice lands; UC-347 shows timeline entries.

## Permissions / Data / External Dependencies
- **Permissions:** CUSTOMER reads own order and own notifications; the escalation itself is written by the system job (ACT-07).
- **Data involved:** order state, `SLA_24H` flag, timeline entry, notification rows, unread badge.
- **External dependencies:** BullMQ notification jobs (BR-PLT-01); admin review queue (B13); deep-link contract (notifications.md §2).

## Acceptance Criteria
- **AC-UC336-01:** Given an order still `CONFIRMED` 24 h after confirmation, when the check runs, then it is escalated to admin review and flagged `SLA_24H` (BR-ORD-10).
- **AC-UC336-02:** Given the escalation, when fan-out runs, then the customer receives a localized in-app notice (FR-017, BR-NTF-04).
- **AC-UC336-03:** Given the escalation, when the timeline loads, then an append-only entry with actor and timestamp appears (BR-ORD-03).
- **AC-UC336-04:** Given the escalation, when further time passes, then the order is never auto-cancelled without a notification (BR-ORD-10).
