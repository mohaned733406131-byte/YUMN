# UC-326 — Re-Reserve Stock When a Cart Line's 15-Minute Countdown Expires

## Header Info
- **Use Case ID:** UC-326
- **Title:** Re-Reserve Stock When a Cart Line's 15-Minute Countdown Expires
- **Actor:** Customer (ACT-01)
- **Trigger:** A cart line's 15-minute reservation countdown reaches zero while the customer is still shopping
- **Priority:** P1
- **Goal:** Release the expired hold cleanly, tell the customer honestly, and let them re-reserve or drop the line.
- **Preconditions:** Cart line carries `reservationExpiresAt = lastActivity + 15 min` (BR-CRT-02, C-13); stock reserved for that line (FR-005).

## Main Scenario
1. The countdown for a line reaches zero; the TTL timer releases the reserved quantity back to available stock (BR-CRT-02, BR-CAT-07).
2. The client refetches the cart on expiry: the line is marked stale/released and its countdown cleared (state-management §4).
3. Returning to the cart, the customer sees the re-reservation prompt (EC-01) instead of a silently held line.
4. Changing quantity or re-adding the line starts a fresh 15-minute reservation (BR-CRT-02) when stock is still available.
5. The countdown restarts and checkout can proceed (UC-011).

## Alternative Scenarios
- **A1 — Stock still available:** re-reservation succeeds on the next add/patch; the line becomes eligible again.
- **A2 — Stock taken by another buyer meanwhile:** `STOCK_UNAVAILABLE`; checkout stays blocked until the line is removed (EC-01, UC-328).
- **A3 — Customer only reads the cart:** `GET /cart` does not refresh the countdown — reads are side-effect free, so expiry still stands.

## Exception Scenarios
- **E1 — Expiry fires mid-checkout:** the stock check re-runs inside the order transaction; failure aborts the saga cleanly with compensating actions (BR-PLT-04).
- **E2 — Release job delayed:** retries under the platform policy with backoff; the released quantity reconciles (BR-PLT-02).
- **E3 — Customer offline at expiry:** on reconnect stale lines are shown and revalidated; no phantom hold survives.

## Postconditions
- Success: a fresh reservation is held with a new deadline; the countdown runs from the server timestamp.
- Failure: the expired hold is released — stock returns to the pool, no orphan reservation, the line is clearly marked stale.

## Business Rules Applied
- BR-CRT-02, BR-CAT-07, BR-PLT-04

## Related Requirements
- FR-010 (Shopping Cart — reservation countdown), FR-005 (Inventory Management — 15-minute TTL)
- **Relation:** UC-010 manages the cart line; UC-328 removes a line when stock is gone; UC-011 reserves at checkout.

## Permissions / Data / External Dependencies
- **Permissions:** CUSTOMER role, owner cart only; stock release happens regardless of the customer's session.
- **Data involved:** cart line reservation deadline, stock `reserved`/`available` counters, stale flags — release + re-reserve.
- **External dependencies:** cart service (B05); inventory service (B02); BullMQ TTL timer job (BR-PLT-01).

## Acceptance Criteria
- **AC-UC326-01:** Given a line whose deadline passed, when the timer fires, then its reserved quantity returns to available stock (BR-CRT-02).
- **AC-UC326-02:** Given an expired line, when the cart is refetched, then the line is flagged stale with no countdown (C-13).
- **AC-UC326-03:** Given stock still available, when the customer re-adds/adjusts the line, then a new 15-minute reservation starts (BR-CRT-02).
- **AC-UC326-04:** Given stock taken meanwhile, when re-reservation is attempted, then `STOCK_UNAVAILABLE` returns and checkout stays blocked (EC-01).
