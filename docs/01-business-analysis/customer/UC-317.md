# UC-317 — Check the Return Policy and Window on the Product Page

## Header Info
- **Use Case ID:** UC-317
- **Title:** Check the Return Policy and Window on the Product Page
- **Actor:** Customer (ACT-01)
- **Trigger:** Customer inspects the return indicator on a product detail page before buying
- **Priority:** P2
- **Goal:** Show the authoritative returnability and window for this product so the customer knows the policy before checkout.
- **Preconditions:** Product detail loaded (UC-007, API-CAT-004 public read); product `ACTIVE` and not deleted (BR-CAT-06).

## Main Scenario
1. Customer opens `/[locale]/p/{product-slug}` and scrolls to the return-policy indicator (FL-02 step 3).
2. Product detail carries `isReturnable` and `returnPeriodDays` from the catalog record (FR-004).
3. The indicator states returnable yes/no and the number of days (C-11 policy surface).
4. Customer reads that the window runs from delivery confirmation date plus `returnPeriodDays`, not from purchase (BR-RET-01).
5. Later, the same values gate the return request on the order (UC-168, FR-016).

## Alternative Scenarios
- **A1 — Product not returnable:** the indicator says so and the return action never appears on future orders (BR-RET-01).
- **A2 — Store default differs:** the product-level values override the store's default policy and are what this page shows.
- **A3 — Guest visitor:** the policy is part of the public product read; no login required.

## Exception Scenarios
- **E1 — Product detail fetch fails:** page error with retry + home/search links; no policy guess is ever rendered.
- **E2 — Product inactive or soft-deleted:** `404` page (BR-CAT-06) — the stale indicator never shows.
- **E3 — Policy changed after purchase:** orders keep the policy captured at placement (`returnPolicyApplied` on order detail), so past orders are unaffected.

## Postconditions
- Success: customer viewed the authoritative policy for this product; read-only — no rows written.
- Failure: no policy shown (never a stale, guessed or other product's policy).

## Business Rules Applied
- BR-RET-01, BR-CAT-06

## Related Requirements
- FR-004 (Product Catalog Management — product policy fields), FR-016 (Returns & Refunds — window enforcement)
- **Relation:** UC-007 is the hosting product page; UC-168 submits the return this policy later gates; UC-169 tracks that request's status.

## Permissions / Data / External Dependencies
- **Permissions:** Public read of active products; the window is enforced per customer only when a return is later requested.
- **Data involved:** product record (`isReturnable`, `returnPeriodDays`), store return-policy defaults, order's captured policy (read later).
- **External dependencies:** catalog service (B02); Redis product cache (NFR-004); no third-party providers.

## Acceptance Criteria
- **AC-UC317-01:** Given a product with `returnPeriodDays = 7`, when the page renders, then the indicator shows a 7-day return window (FR-004).
- **AC-UC317-02:** Given `isReturnable = false`, when the page renders, then the indicator states the product is not returnable (BR-RET-01).
- **AC-UC317-03:** Given an inactive product, when its URL is opened, then a 404 page renders with no policy content (BR-CAT-06).
- **AC-UC317-04:** Given the same product after a policy update, when a past order opens, then the order still shows the policy captured at placement (FR-016).
