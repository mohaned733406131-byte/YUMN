# UC-328 — Remove an Ineligible Line That Blocks Checkout

## Header Info
- **Use Case ID:** UC-328
- **Title:** Remove an Ineligible Line That Blocks Checkout
- **Actor:** Customer (ACT-01)
- **Trigger:** Checkout reports one or more lines as ineligible (inactive, out of stock, or out of policy)
- **Priority:** P1
- **Goal:** Clear the blockers explicitly — no silent drops — so checkout can proceed with a clean cart.
- **Preconditions:** Checkout-view returned `blockers[]` with `flags.ineligible` lines (BR-CRT-05); cart belongs to the caller (FR-010).

## Main Scenario
1. Customer opens checkout; `blockers[]` lists every ineligible line with its reason (BR-CRT-05).
2. UI highlights the lines with "remove to continue" and keeps confirm disabled (state-management §4).
3. Customer taps remove on a blocked line → `DELETE /cart/items/{id}` (conceptual).
4. The line is removed and its reserved stock is released immediately (BR-CRT-02); blockers are recomputed server-side.
5. With `blockers[]` empty, checkout proceeds to the address step (FR-011).

## Alternative Scenarios
- **A1 — Line restocked/reactivated before removal:** revalidation clears the flag and checkout proceeds without a removal (BR-CRT-05).
- **A2 — Several blockers:** each must be cleared; confirm stays disabled until none remain — no partial silent drop (EC-07).
- **A3 — Customer empties the cart instead:** `DELETE /cart` leads to the empty-cart state with browse/search recovery (UC-160).

## Exception Scenarios
- **E1 — Product deactivated again right after revalidation:** the session start fails with `CART_ITEM_INELIGIBLE`; no order row exists (BR-CAT-06).
- **E2 — Remove request fails (network):** the line and blocker stay put; retrying the idempotent delete is safe.
- **E3 — Last line removed:** the empty-cart state renders with recovery actions — never a blank screen (NFR-012).

## Postconditions
- Success: cart has zero blockers, removed line's reservation released, remaining lines untouched; checkout can advance.
- Failure: cart unchanged, no order row created, no stock leaked or double-released.

## Business Rules Applied
- BR-CRT-05, BR-CRT-02, BR-CAT-06

## Related Requirements
- FR-010 (Shopping Cart — ineligible items block checkout)
- **Relation:** UC-010 manages lines; UC-325 covers limit guards; UC-011 resumes once blockers are cleared.

## Permissions / Data / External Dependencies
- **Permissions:** CUSTOMER role, owner cart only; the eligibility verdict itself is server-side.
- **Data involved:** cart lines, `blockers[]`, `flags.ineligible`, stock reservation for the removed line.
- **External dependencies:** cart service (B05); inventory release job (B02); checkout session (B05).

## Acceptance Criteria
- **AC-UC328-01:** Given an inactive product in the cart, when checkout opens, then it appears in `blockers[]` and confirm is disabled (BR-CRT-05).
- **AC-UC328-02:** Given a blocked line, when it is removed, then its reserved stock is released immediately (BR-CRT-02).
- **AC-UC328-03:** Given blockers remain, when confirm is attempted, then checkout is refused and no order row exists (BR-CRT-05).
- **AC-UC328-04:** Given all blockers cleared, when checkout recomputes, then the session advances to the address step.
