# UC-180 — View Own Support Ticket List and Status

## Header Info
- **Use Case ID:** UC-180
- **Title:** View Own Support Ticket List and Status
- **Actor:** Customer (ACT-01)
- **Trigger:** Customer opens Help → "My tickets"
- **Priority:** P2
- **Goal:** Show the customer their own support tickets with number, status and last-update so they can follow support cases at a glance.
- **Preconditions:** Authenticated; tickets may not exist yet (A2); list is owner-scoped (`roles=CUSTOMER` on API-ADM-036).

## Main Scenario
1. Customer opens Help → My Tickets → `GET /support/tickets` (API-ADM-036), a cursor feed of own tickets only.
2. Each row shows `number` (`SUP-NNNNNN`), subject, status `OPEN|IN_PROGRESS|RESOLVED|CLOSED`, `updatedAt` and `unreadFromSupport`.
3. The unread badge signals a new staff reply carried through the in-app channel (BR-NTF-01 channel set: SMS, WhatsApp, in-app, push).
4. Customer filters with `?status` and pages forward via cursor.
5. Opening one ticket (thread, linked order, timeline) belongs to the contact-support flow (cross-reference: UC-014 step3 — detail only).

## Alternative Scenarios
- **A1 — Filtered view:** `?status=IN_PROGRESS` narrows to pending cases.
- **A2 — Empty list:** no tickets yet — zero items, no placeholder rows.
- **A3 — Resolved/closed ticket:** listed with its final status; reply/reopen detail is UC-014's scope.

## Exception Scenarios
- **E1 — Invalid or expired session:** `AUTH_INVALID`; client redirects to login.
- **E2 — Invalid cursor or status filter:** `VALIDATION_ERROR`; no data returned.
- **E3 — Read failure:** error state; no partial list is rendered.

## Postconditions
- Success: customer sees their own ticket statuses; read-only — no ticket, thread or status change.
- Failure: no data rendered; session either invalid (→ login) or intact.

## Business Rules Applied
- BR-NTF-01

## Related Requirements
- FR-020 (Platform Administration — support tickets, customer view)
- **Relation:** UC-014 step3 (detail only) — creating tickets and reading the thread live there; this use case is the list/status projection.

## Permissions / Data / External Dependencies
- **Permissions:** customer reads own tickets only; staff queues are admin/moderator-scoped (separate roles).
- **Data involved:** ticket summary rows (number, subject, status, updatedAt, unread flag) — read-only.
- **External dependencies:** support service (B13); cursor pagination; in-app notification badge (B10).

## Acceptance Criteria
- **AC-UC180-01:** Given 4 own tickets, when the feed loads, then all 4 appear with number, status and `updatedAt` (FR-020).
- **AC-UC180-02:** Given a pending staff reply, when listed, then `unreadFromSupport` is `true` (BR-NTF-01).
- **AC-UC180-03:** Given other users' tickets exist, when the feed is requested, then only the caller's tickets are returned.
- **AC-UC180-04:** Given `?status=RESOLVED`, when applied, then only resolved tickets are listed.
