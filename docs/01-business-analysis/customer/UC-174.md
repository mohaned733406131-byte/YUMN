# UC-174 — View Escrow Holding Status for Own Orders

## Header Info
- **Use Case ID:** UC-174
- **Title:** View Escrow Holding Status for Own Orders
- **Actor:** Customer (ACT-01)
- **Trigger:** Customer opens the escrow/hold section of their order detail
- **Priority:** P2
- **Goal:** Show the customer how much of their payment is held in escrow per sub-order, until when, and why a hold is frozen or released.
- **Preconditions:** Authenticated; customer owns the order; escrow rows may be absent (not delivered yet — A2); read-only access.

## Main Scenario
1. From order detail (cross-reference: UC-012 step6 — countdown) customer opens escrow status → `GET /wallet/escrow?orderId` (API-WAL-009).
2. System returns per sub-order rows: `amountHeld` (integer YER), state, `deliveredAt`, `releaseEligibleAt`, `releasedAt`, `frozenBy: NONE|DISPUTE|RETURN`, plus held/released totals.
3. On `DELIVERED` the funds for the sub-order are held for 7 days (BR-ESC-01); the displayed countdown ends at `releaseEligibleAt`.
4. Release requires 7 days elapsed AND no active dispute AND state outside {DISPUTED, RETURN_*, REFUNDED} (BR-ESC-02); the view explains why a row is not yet eligible.
5. The screen is read-only and owner-scoped to the customer's own orders.

## Alternative Scenarios
- **A1 — Frozen by dispute/return:** `frozenBy` names the hold reason; release waits until the case clears (BR-ESC-02).
- **A2 — Not delivered yet:** no escrow rows — empty list with zero totals is valid.
- **A3 — Released:** `releasedAt` set and the amount appears in the released total (BR-ESC-02).

## Exception Scenarios
- **E1 — Foreign order or sub-order:** `NOT_FOUND`/timeline access denied (owner scope).
- **E2 — Invalid filter value:** `VALIDATION_ERROR`; no rows returned.
- **E3 — Invalid or expired session:** `AUTH_INVALID`; client redirects to login.

## Postconditions
- Success: customer sees exact held/released amounts in integer YER and the release eligibility; read-only — no escrow or ledger change.
- Failure: no data rendered; escrow untouched.

## Business Rules Applied
- BR-ESC-01, BR-ESC-02

## Related Requirements
- FR-014 (Escrow, Commission & Vendor Payouts — customer-visible hold status)
- **Relation:** UC-012 step6 (countdown) — order tracking surfaces the 7-day escrow countdown detailed here.

## Permissions / Data / External Dependencies
- **Permissions:** customer reads escrow of own orders only; vendors read their own sub-order projection (separate role).
- **Data involved:** escrow hold rows, amounts (integer YER), freeze reason, release timestamps — read-only.
- **External dependencies:** escrow service (B07) only.

## Acceptance Criteria
- **AC-UC174-01:** Given a sub-order delivered at T, when read at T+2 days, then `releaseEligibleAt` = T+7 days and `amountHeld` > 0 in integer YER (BR-ESC-01).
- **AC-UC174-02:** Given an open dispute on the sub-order, when read, then `frozenBy = DISPUTE` and it is not released (BR-ESC-02).
- **AC-UC174-03:** Given 7 days elapsed with no dispute in a completing state, when release runs, then `releasedAt` is set (BR-ESC-02).
- **AC-UC174-04:** Given another customer's order, when requested, then access is denied.
