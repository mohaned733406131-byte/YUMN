# UC-360 — Rate the Support Resolution in the Post-Ticket Survey

## Header Info
- **Use Case ID:** UC-360
- **Title:** Rate the Support Resolution in the Post-Ticket Survey
- **Actor:** Customer (ACT-01)
- **Trigger:** The customer's support ticket reaches `RESOLVED` and the micro-survey is offered
- **Priority:** P2
- **Goal:** Capture a single-question satisfaction score on the resolution without blocking the customer.
- **Preconditions:** Ticket resolved by support (FR-020); survey offered once per resolution (feedback §9 CSAT micro-survey); customer's locale known (BR-NTF-04).

## Main Scenario
1. The ticket moves to `RESOLVED` → the customer sees the resolution note in the thread (API-ADM-041).
2. A CSAT micro-survey appears: one question, 1–5 rating plus an optional comment (feedback §9).
3. The survey renders in both locales — Arabic default, English parity (BR-PLT-05, NFR-013).
4. The customer submits the score → it is recorded against the ticket resolution for completion tracking.
5. The customer can dismiss it — the survey never blocks closing the ticket or reading the thread.

## Alternative Scenarios
- **A1 — Dismiss without rating:** the survey closes; the thread remains readable — no re-prompt loop (feedback §9 once-per-resolution posture).
- **A2 — Low score with comment:** the comment is captured as typed; handling of follow-up is an internal concern — no bot exists (scope lock, feedback §8).
- **A3 — Reopened then resolved again:** the survey is offered for the new resolution only (API-ADM-041 reopen-then-resolve path).

## Exception Scenarios
- **E1 — Submission fails:** retry with the standard error pattern; the entered score is not silently discarded.
- **E2 — Ticket not resolved:** the survey is never shown — it exists only for `RESOLVED` tickets.
- **E3 — Session expired:** re-auth prompt, then the thread reopens; the survey state is re-checked server-side.

## Postconditions
- Success: the rating (and optional comment) is stored for that resolution; the thread is unchanged.
- Failure: no score recorded; no duplicate survey for the same resolution; no data beyond the typed comment is collected (data minimization).

## Business Rules Applied
- BR-PLT-05

## Related Requirements
- FR-020 (Platform Administration, Settings & Audit — support lifecycle)
- **Relation:** UC-359 appends to the thread being rated; UC-348 creates the lockout ticket; UC-342/UC-014 open tickets whose resolutions are rated here.

## Permissions / Data / External Dependencies
- **Permissions:** CUSTOMER role, own resolved ticket only — ratings cannot be attached to foreign tickets (ownership scope).
- **Data involved:** survey response (score 1–5, optional comment), ticket id, resolution reference — minimal PII (data minimization).
- **External dependencies:** support ticketing module (B13); completion/quality feedback loop (`21-completion/`, feedback §9); localized survey copy (BR-NTF-04).

## Acceptance Criteria
- **AC-UC360-01:** Given a `RESOLVED` ticket, when the thread opens, then the one-question 1–5 survey with optional comment shows (feedback §9).
- **AC-UC360-02:** Given a submitted rating, when the request commits, then exactly one score is stored for that resolution.
- **AC-UC360-03:** Given an unresolved ticket, when the thread opens, then no survey is shown (FR-020).
- **AC-UC360-04:** Given either locale, when the survey renders, then it displays fully localized — Arabic default, English parity (BR-PLT-05, NFR-013).
