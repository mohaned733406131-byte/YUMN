# UC-340 — Poll a Top-Up Awaiting Provider Confirmation

## Header Info
- **Use Case ID:** UC-340
- **Title:** Poll a Top-Up Awaiting Provider Confirmation
- **Actor:** Customer (ACT-01)
- **Trigger:** An m-Floos/OneCash top-up sits at `PENDING_PROVIDER` after the provider hand-off
- **Priority:** P1
- **Goal:** Watch the pending top-up until a verified callback credits the wallet — never claim credit from the client.
- **Preconditions:** Top-up initiated with an idempotency key (FR-013); provider rail chosen (C-05); status endpoint readable (API-WAL-004).

## Main Scenario
1. After `POST /wallet/topups` the top-up is `PENDING_PROVIDER`; the screen shows "بانتظار التأكيد من المزوّد / awaiting provider confirmation".
2. Client polls `GET /wallet/topups/{id}` for the owner's record while the screen is visible (API-WAL-004).
3. Credit posts only after a verified provider callback or a reconciled poll — never on client claim (BR-PAY-03).
4. On credit: balanced ledger rows post (BR-PAY-06), the toast + balance update and the new transaction row appear (screen-states §9).
5. After 15 minutes without confirmation: "still checking" plus a support link — no manual "credit me" button (screen-states §6).

## Alternative Scenarios
- **A1 — Customer leaves the screen:** the pending intent stays on the wallet; polling resumes on return.
- **A2 — Callback already landed:** the first poll returns `CREDITED`; credit happens exactly once (edge-cases EC-20).
- **A3 — Bank-transfer rail instead:** status is `PENDING_VERIFICATION` → the slip-upload path (UC-341).

## Exception Scenarios
- **E1 — Provider rejects:** `REJECTED` with reason and "try another method" — only the three `C-05` rails are offered.
- **E2 — Provider outage:** the pending state persists and polling continues; the wallet stays untouched (NFR-007).
- **E3 — Duplicate callback:** idempotent — the second delivery is a no-op with one credit (BR-PAY-08).

## Postconditions
- Success: wallet credited exactly once with balanced ledger rows; the top-up shows `CREDITED` with timestamp.
- Failure: no credit, no ledger movement; the pending intent remains intact and resumable.

## Business Rules Applied
- BR-PAY-03, BR-PAY-06, BR-PAY-08

## Related Requirements
- FR-013 (Wallet & Payment Processing — provider top-up lifecycle)
- **Relation:** UC-041 initiates the top-up; UC-341 covers the bank-transfer branch; UC-339 shows the resulting ledger rows.

## Permissions / Data / External Dependencies
- **Permissions:** CUSTOMER role, own top-up record only — credit is written by the system/provider path, never by the client.
- **Data involved:** top-up intent, status, provider callback, ledger rows, balance — read plus system-written credit.
- **External dependencies:** m-Floos/OneCash providers (DEP-05, INT-REQ-001); reconciliation poll; BullMQ jobs (BR-PLT-01).

## Acceptance Criteria
- **AC-UC340-01:** Given a `PENDING_PROVIDER` top-up, when polled, then the pending status returns and no credit exists yet (BR-PAY-03).
- **AC-UC340-02:** Given a verified callback, when credit posts, then exactly one balanced debit/credit pair exists (BR-PAY-06).
- **AC-UC340-03:** Given 15 minutes without confirmation, when the screen renders, then "still checking" plus a support link shows with no manual credit button (screen-states §6).
- **AC-UC340-04:** Given a duplicate callback, when it arrives, then it is a no-op and the balance increases once (BR-PAY-08).
