# TC-018 — Checkout reserves stock atomically and the 15-minute TTL releases it

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Objective | Verify that creating an order moves `stock` into `reservedQuantity` atomically, that the 15-minute reservation TTL releases it on payment timeout, and that concurrent checkouts never oversell. |
| Level | integration |
| Priority | P1 |
| Automation | Yes — Jest + Supertest (with stock ledger assertions) |

## Preconditions
- Product `SKU-A1` has `stock: 10`, `reservedQuantity: 0`.
- Two carts (customer A and customer B) each hold 6 units of `SKU-A1`.
- The reservation-TTL worker is controllable (backdate `reservedUntil` in tests).

## Test Data

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SKU | `SKU-A1` |
| Initial stock | `10` |
| Each cart quantity | `6` (two carts → total demand 12 > 10) |
| Reservation TTL | 15 minutes (`C-13`) |

## Steps
1. `POST /checkout/session` from customer A with 6 units and complete the order (`POST /orders`).
2. Read `GET /store/inventory/SKU-A1`.
3. `POST /checkout/session` from customer B with 6 units simultaneously (parallel requests).
4. Inspect both responses and the stock ledger rows.
5. Backdate the reservation so `reservedUntil` is in the past, then run/trigger the TTL worker.
6. Re-read the inventory row and the ledger.

## Expected Result
- Step 1 succeeds; after the order exists the row shows `stock: 4`, `reservedQuantity: 6`, `reservedUntil = now + 15 min` — stock is never silently decremented before payment.
- Step 3: exactly one of A/B succeeds; the loser receives `409 STOCK_CONFLICT` or `422 INSUFFICIENT_STOCK` with the available quantity in `details`.
- Step 4: two ledger entries exist for the winner (`RESERVE` + `COMMIT/RELEASE` paths), and the aggregate invariant holds: `stock ≥ reservedQuantity ≥ 0`, never negative (check constraint `C-11`).
- No interleaving produces `stock = -2` or a double reservation — the write is atomic (`SELECT … FOR UPDATE` / single-row update).
- Step 5: after the TTL fires, `reservedQuantity` returns to `0`, `stock` returns to `10`, and a `RELEASE_TTL` ledger row records actor `SYSTEM` with a timestamp (`BR-INV-04`).
- Once released, customer B can now complete a fresh checkout of 6 units.
- The customer whose reservation expired receives an order-status update to `CANCELLED`/timeout handling (see TC-031 side effects) with no wallet debit retained.
