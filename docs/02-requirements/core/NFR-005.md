# NFR-005 — Uptime

## Description

The platform must be available **99.99% of the time** — at most **4.32 minutes of downtime per calendar month** (≤ 52.6 minutes per year), measured over any rolling 30-day window. Availability is judged from the customer's perspective: external probes against public endpoints, backed by internal liveness/readiness health checks (`BR-PLT-07`) that gate traffic at the load balancer. Planned maintenance counts against the same budget unless separately approved and announced.

## Metric / Target

- **Availability ≥ 99.99%** on a rolling 30-day window = downtime ≤ **4.32 min/month** (`C-26`, `AC-S-06`).
- Probe coverage: 60-second interval external HTTP probes on home, product, login, health endpoints — 100% of public critical routes.
- Error-budget policy: page on-call when 30-day budget burn rate implies > 2× the allowed downtime; alert within **1 minute** of probe failure (2 consecutive failed probes).
- Health endpoints `/healthz` (liveness) and `/readyz` (readiness) respond in < 1 s and are wired to LB target health.
- Mean time to detect (MTTD) ≤ 1 minute; mean time to restore (MTTR) tracked against the 1-hour RTO (NFR-006).

## Rationale

`C-26` fixes 99.99% availability as non-negotiable; `OBJ-05` (Critical) and `AC-S-06` make it a post-launch verified SLO. Wallet payments, escrow (`C-12`) and order lifecycle (`C-09`) make downtime immediately costly: customers cannot pay, couriers cannot confirm deliveries, vendors cannot ship.

## Measurement Method

- **Prometheus `blackbox_exporter`** probes every 60 s + an independent external uptime monitor (catches monitoring-plane failure).
- Uptime computed monthly as `1 - (sum(probe_failures) / total_probes)` in Grafana SLO dashboard; downtime incidents cross-checked against incident records.
- Quarterly review of probe results and error-budget reports in `../../12-non-functional/core/reliability.md`.

## Verification / Acceptance

`AC-NFR-005-01` — rolling 30-day availability ≥ 99.99% post-launch per the SLO dashboard (`AC-S-06`). `AC-NFR-005-02` — probe coverage of all critical routes complete, alert fires within 1 minute of simulated outage, and the error-budget burn alert is armed. Both PASS required for operational sign-off.

## Dependencies

- NFR-006 (recovery after outage), NFR-007 (degradation instead of outage), NFR-014 (alerting), NFR-020 (health endpoints/runbooks).
- FR-013, FR-014 (money paths whose downtime is most damaging), DEP-08 (domain/TLS/CDN).

## Failure Consequences

Downtime beyond 4.32 min/month breaches `C-26` — a headline trust failure for a wallet-based marketplace; orders and deliveries stall; vendors lose sales; `OBJ-05`/`AC-S-06` fail and the platform's operational credibility with vendors and regulators is damaged.
