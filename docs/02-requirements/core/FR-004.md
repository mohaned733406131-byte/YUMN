# FR-004 — Product Catalog Management

**Block:** B02 Product Catalog · **Priority:** Critical · **Status:** approved · **Registry:** `DOC-REQ-001` §1

FR-004 covers authoring and publishing of the marketplace catalog: products with Arabic-first content, variants (≤5 dimensions, ≤50 combinations), a 5-level category tree, custom attributes, images (≤10 per product, ≤5 MB, jpg/png/webp, EXIF stripped), and YER-only pricing with sale-price validation. Products are soft-deleted, only ACTIVE products reach search/storefront, publishing requires an APPROVED-KYC store, and each product carries its merchant-configurable return policy fields.

## Rationale

- Traces `OBJ-01` (block B02 production-ready), `OBJ-03` (Arabic-first quality — Arabic product name is mandatory) and `OBJ-12` (`C-04`, `C-11`, `C-24` compliance).
- Catalog is the demand-side foundation of `OBJ-11` (marketplace liquidity): without valid, publishable listings there is nothing to search (FR-009), cart (FR-010) or order (FR-011).
- Validation up front prevents downstream money bugs: price rules (`BR-CAT-04`) feed order bounds `C-14`, and return-policy fields (`C-11`) feed FR-016.

## Requirements Detail

- Create/edit/soft-delete products; publish requires Arabic name, price in YER, category, ≥1 image, stock ≥0 and a store (`BR-CAT-01`).
- Variants: ≤5 dimensions, ≤50 combinations, SKU unique within the store (`BR-CAT-02`).
- Category tree depth ≤5 levels with slugs unique per level (`BR-CAT-03`).
- Price > 0; sale price strictly < original price; amounts stored as integer YER (`BR-CAT-04`, `BR-PAY-10`, `C-04`).
- Physical goods only — subscription, trial, sample and rental types are rejected at validation (`BR-CAT-05`).
- Soft-delete only; inactive/deleted products are excluded from search and storefront (`BR-CAT-06`).
- Images: ≤10 per product, ≤5 MB each, jpg/png/webp only, EXIF stripped on upload, malware scanned, no SVG execution (`BR-CAT-08`, `SEC-REQ-011`).
- Custom attribute key/values attach to products, scoped by category for consistent filtering (feeds FR-009).
- Return-policy fields per product: `isReturnable`, `returnPeriodDays` (`C-11`).
- Publishing is blocked until the owning store's KYC status is APPROVED (`BR-VND-01`).
- Product content and validation messages localized ar (default) / en (`BR-PLT-05`, `C-24`).
- Every catalog mutation is ownership-scoped to the vendor's `store_id` (`BR-VND-07`).

## Preconditions

- The vendor account exists with an approved store (FR-007/FR-008) for publishing; drafts may exist before approval but cannot go ACTIVE.
- Category tree and storage (`DEP-07`) are available; image size/type validation is configured.
- Stock values exist (managed by FR-005) before a product can be published as available.

## Expected Result

A vendor can fully describe and publish a compliant product that is immediately valid for search, cart and checkout; non-compliant submissions are rejected with localized, field-level errors; no deleted/inactive or non-approved-KYC product is ever visible to customers.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **AC-FR004-01** — Given a product with no Arabic name, when publish is attempted, then validation fails and no ACTIVE product exists.
- **AC-FR004-02** — Given a variant definition with 6 dimensions, when saved, then validation rejects it (max 5 dimensions).
- **AC-FR004-03** — Given a 12 MB JPEG or any SVG upload, when the image is submitted, then the upload is rejected per `BR-CAT-08`/`SEC-REQ-011`.
- **AC-FR004-04** — Given a soft-deleted product, when storefront or search queries run, then it never appears in results.
- **AC-FR004-05** — Given a product violating any catalog rule (price ≤ 0, sale ≥ original, 6th category level, duplicate SKU in store), when it is saved, then the specific validation error is returned and no row persists (`BR-CAT-02/03/04`).

## Business Rules Applied

- `BR-CAT-01` publishable-product requirements; `BR-CAT-02` variant/SKU limits; `BR-CAT-03` category depth/slug uniqueness.
- `BR-CAT-04` price and sale-price validation; `BR-CAT-05` physical goods only; `BR-CAT-06` soft-delete and ACTIVE-only visibility.
- `BR-CAT-08` image limits and EXIF stripping; `BR-VND-01` KYC gate for publishing; `BR-PAY-10` integer YER amounts; `BR-PLT-05` bilingual content.

## Constraints Honored

- `C-04` single currency — all prices in YER, no USD/SAR pricing.
- `C-11` merchant-configurable return policy stored per product/store.
- `C-24` Arabic-first content (mandatory Arabic name) with English parity.

## Dependencies

- **FR:** FR-005 (stock), FR-007 (KYC gate), FR-008 (store/branding), FR-009 (indexing), FR-016 (return policy consumption), FR-019 (merchandising links).
- **NFR/SEC/DEP:** SEC-REQ-011, NFR-013, NFR-004 (catalog read caching), DEP-07 (object storage).

## Verification Method

- Unit tests: every validation rule (publish requirements, variant dims, price rules, product types, image constraints).
- Integration tests: draft → publish with KYC gate; soft-delete visibility; upload security (type/size/EXIF/malware).
- E2E: vendor lists a product and it appears in storefront/search in both locales (supports `OBJ-06` <10 min listing target, `NFR-012`).

## Out of Scope Notes

- Digital/downloadable goods and services; subscription, trial, sample and rental product types (excluded by `BR-CAT-05`, project scope).
- Multi-currency pricing and tax-inclusive price display toggles beyond VAT rules (`C-04`, `BR-FIN-01`).
- Bulk CSV/product-import tooling, product duplication and AI-generated listings — not specified in v1; sponsored placements are FUTURE SCOPE.
