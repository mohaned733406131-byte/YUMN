# NFR-019 — Legal/data

## Description

The platform must operate within its legal and data-protection envelope: **PDPA-aligned controls** (data minimisation, purpose limitation, retention and deletion, user rights over personal data), **VAT calculated on every order**, and **financial/audit records retained for at least 5 years**. Compliance is demonstrated with evidence — checklists, automated tests and sign-offs — not asserted. Open legal positions (`ASM-09…ASM-13`, `DEP-09`, `DEP-10`) must be resolved before launch.

## Metric / Target

- **VAT coverage: 100% of orders** — VAT = 15% × (subtotal − coupon discount), shipping untaxed, added on top and shown in every price breakdown (`BR-FIN-01`); boundary tests (0 discount, 90% discount, free-shipping coupon, multi-vendor split) pass 100%.
- **Audit retention ≥ 5 years** for financial records and privileged-action audit entries (`BR-PLT-06`, `SEC-REQ-010`); retention job evidence shows 0 records inside the window deleted before 5 years.
- **PDPA-aligned control checklist: 100% implemented** — data minimisation (`DATA-REQ-002`), retention/deletion workflow (`DATA-REQ-003`), account-deletion request fulfilled within the published SLA, data export/erasure rights path documented and testable.
- Personal data encrypted at rest (AES-256 for PII/financial fields, `SEC-REQ-006`); phone numbers never logged in plaintext — 0 occurrences in log audit.
- **Legal sign-offs obtained** for all open assumptions (`AC-S-24`) before production launch.

## Rationale

`AC-S-24` (legal/compliance sign-offs) is a launch gate; `NFR-017` and `DATA-REQ-003` set the 5-year horizon; `OBJ-08` requires auditable operations. VAT is a statutory obligation on every transaction (`BR-FIN-01`), and closed-loop wallet operation depends on `DEP-10` (Central Bank position) — existential for `C-01`.

## Measurement Method

- Automated **VAT calculation test suite** over boundary matrix; monthly sample audit of real order breakdowns.
- **Compliance checklist review** (PDPA-aligned controls mapped to implemented features/tests) signed by the compliance owner.
- Retention-job dry-run reports + SQL counts of records older than 5 years; log-audit script for plaintext PII.
- Sign-off records filed for `ASM-09…ASM-13` resolutions.

## Verification / Acceptance

`AC-NFR-019-01` — VAT tests 100% green across the boundary matrix and sampled production-like orders show correct VAT and breakdowns. `AC-NFR-019-02` — retention evidence proves ≥ 5-year financial/audit retention, the PDPA checklist is 100% implemented, and all legal sign-offs (`AC-S-24`) are on file. Both PASS before launch.

## Dependencies

- NFR-008 (integrity of the records being retained), NFR-014 (audit logging), NFR-017 (storage to hold 5 years), NFR-020 (retention jobs operable).
- FR-013, FR-020, DATA-REQ-002, DATA-REQ-003, SEC-REQ-010, DEP-09, DEP-10.

## Failure Consequences

Statutory exposure on VAT and data protection; inability to produce financial records for disputes or regulators; `AC-S-24` fails and launch is blocked; worst case, `DEP-10` rules the wallet-only model (`C-01`) non-compliant — a project-ending outcome.
