# FR-006 — Reviews & Ratings

**Block:** B02 Product Catalog · **Priority:** High · **Status:** approved · **Registry:** `DOC-REQ-001` §1

FR-006 lets only verified buyers rate their purchased items: 1–5 integer stars with up to 5 images, written strictly after DELIVERED within a 30-day window, one review per order item, editable once within 7 days. Vendors may respond once per review; Moderators/Admins may hide reviews with an audit entry; store ratings are recomputed incrementally from visible reviews only.

## Rationale

- Traces `OBJ-01` (block B02) and `OBJ-11` (marketplace liquidity — reviews are the trust signal that drives repeat purchase).
- Gated, post-delivery reviews protect vendors from fake/competitor abuse while giving customers a buyer-protected voice; both sides of `OBJ-02`'s trust model.
- Moderation with audit (`BR-REV-04`) is required because yumn hosts third-party content (abuse/inappropriate material) — platform liability concern.

## Requirements Detail

- Only the purchasing customer may review, and only after the order reaches DELIVERED; window = 30 days from delivery (`BR-REV-01`).
- One review per order item; editable exactly once within 7 days of creation (`BR-REV-02`).
- Rating is an integer 1–5; ≤5 images, ≤5 MB each, subject to upload security (type validation, EXIF strip, no SVG) (`BR-REV-03`, `SEC-REQ-011`).
- Vendor may respond exactly once per review; response is localized ar/en (`BR-REV-04`, `BR-PLT-05`).
- Moderator/Admin may hide a review for abuse/inappropriateness with an audit entry (`BR-REV-04`, `BR-PLT-06`).
- Customers may report a review for moderation review (`Moderator` permission in `actors-and-roles.md`).
- Store rating = average of **visible** product ratings with review count shown; recomputation is incremental via background job (`BR-REV-05`, `BR-PLT-01`).
- Hidden reviews are excluded from averages and counts once recompute runs.
- Review eligibility is validated server-side against the order/item state (verified purchase) — UI affordances are UX only (`SEC-REQ-004`).
- Review lists support pagination and localized rendering of Arabic content (RTL) (`C-24`, `NFR-013`).
- Review text/images are scoped so a customer sees their own pending edits and others see published content (`DATA-REQ-008`).

## Preconditions

- The customer owns an order containing the item; the order state is DELIVERED (FR-012/FR-015) and the 30-day window has not elapsed.
- The product exists and is visible (FR-004); storage (`DEP-07`) accepts review images within limits.
- No prior review exists for the same order item (or an in-window edit is being made).

## Expected Result

Eligible buyers create compliant reviews that display publicly with verified-purchase provenance; ineligible attempts are rejected with clear localized reasons; vendor responses and moderation actions apply exactly once and are audited; store/product ratings reflect only visible reviews and update incrementally.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **AC-FR006-01** — Given an order in CONFIRMED state, when the customer submits a review, then it is rejected until the order is DELIVERED.
- **AC-FR006-02** — Given delivery 31 days ago, when a review is submitted, then it is rejected (window elapsed).
- **AC-FR006-03** — Given a rating of 6 or 6 attached images, when submitted, then validation fails and nothing is stored.
- **AC-FR006-04** — Given a review hidden by a Moderator, when the rating recompute job runs, then it is excluded from the average and the visible count.

## Business Rules Applied

- `BR-REV-01` purchaser-only, post-DELIVERED, 30-day window; `BR-REV-02` one per order item, single edit in 7 days.
- `BR-REV-03` integer 1–5 rating, ≤5 images ≤5 MB; `BR-REV-04` one vendor response, hide with audit.
- `BR-REV-05` store rating from visible reviews, incremental recompute job.

## Constraints Honored

- `C-24` Arabic-first localized review UI and bilingual vendor responses (no other numbered constraint applies directly to this FR).

## Dependencies

- **FR:** FR-004 (product), FR-012 (DELIVERED state gate), FR-015 (delivery confirmation), FR-020 (moderation/audit console).
- **NFR/SEC/DEP:** SEC-REQ-011, NFR-004 (cached aggregates), NFR-013, DEP-07 (image storage).

## Verification Method

- Unit tests: eligibility window arithmetic (delivery +30 days), rating bounds, edit-once logic, response-once logic, average computation with hidden reviews.
- Integration tests: verified-purchase enforcement, moderation hide/unhide with audit row, incremental recompute job.
- E2E/abuse tests: reviewer attempts cross-order review, oversized image upload, and post-window submission all rejected.

## Out of Scope Notes

- Video reviews, review polling/helpfulness votes, incentivized or compensated reviews, and review editing beyond the single 7-day edit.
- AI-generated review summaries or sentiment analysis (no AI features in v1 scope).
- Per-vendor separate ratings beyond the store rating derived from product reviews (`BR-REV-05`).
