# FR-007 — Vendor Onboarding & KYC

**Block:** B03 Store Management · **Priority:** Critical · **Status:** approved · **Registry:** `DOC-REQ-001` §1

FR-007 takes a vendor from application to selling: phone-based registration (FR-001), one store per vendor, secure KYC document submission, admin review decided within 48 hours, then APPROVED / REJECTED (resubmission allowed) — plus suspension of existing vendors with well-defined freeze effects. Products cannot be published before approval, and payouts require APPROVED KYC with a non-suspended store.

## Rationale

- Traces `OBJ-06` (first vendor live within 48 h; KYC decision SLA ≤ 48 h) and `OBJ-01` (block B03) — vendor supply is the marketplace's critical path (`OBJ-11`).
- Enforces `BR-VND-01`/`BR-VND-03`: without a hard publishing gate and a decision SLA, either unvetted sellers or stalled onboarding would break the trust model.
- Suspension semantics (`BR-VND-04`) protect buyers and the escrow model when a vendor goes non-compliant mid-flight.

## Requirements Detail

- Vendor registration uses phone + password + OTP (FR-001, `C-06`); exactly one store per vendor in v1 (`BR-VND-02`).
- KYC application collects the required document set configured in platform settings and submits it through secure upload (type/size validation, malware scan, no SVG, EXIF strip) (`SEC-REQ-011`).
- KYC status lifecycle: submitted → under review → APPROVED or REJECTED; rejection allows unlimited resubmission (`BR-VND-03`).
- Every decision must be made within 48 hours of submission; overdue cases escalate/alert the review queue (`BR-VND-03`, `OBJ-06`).
- No product publishing until KYC = APPROVED (`BR-VND-01`); draft catalog work is allowed pre-approval.
- Suspension of an approved vendor: products hidden from storefront, new orders blocked, existing orders frozen, payouts held pending review (`BR-VND-04`).
- Payout eligibility requires KYC = APPROVED and a non-suspended store (`BR-ESC-06`).
- Owner may invite/remove staff (Viewer/Editor/Manager) only after store creation (`BR-VND-06`).
- Decision outcomes are notified to the vendor by SMS/WhatsApp/in-app in the vendor's locale (`BR-NTF-04`, `FR-017`).
- KYC decisions and suspensions write append-only audit entries with actor and reason (`BR-PLT-06`, `SEC-REQ-010`).
- Vendors' queries and applications are ownership-scoped (`BR-VND-07`).

## Preconditions

- FR-001 authentication (phone + OTP) is operational (`DEP-06`).
- The admin KYC queue exists (FR-020) with reviewers holding KYC-approval permission (`actors-and-roles.md` permission matrix).
- Object storage (`DEP-07`) accepts KYC documents within configured limits.

## Expected Result

A vendor can apply, submit documents, receive a decision within 48 hours, and — if approved — configure a store and publish products; rejected vendors can resubmit; suspended vendors are immediately contained across storefront, ordering and payouts, with every decision audited and communicated.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **AC-FR007-01** — Given a submitted KYC application, when 48 hours elapse without a decision, then the case is escalated/alerted so the SLA breach is visible.
- **AC-FR007-02** — Given KYC status ≠ APPROVED, when the vendor attempts to publish a product, then publishing is denied.
- **AC-FR007-03** — Given an approved vendor is suspended, then products are hidden, new orders are blocked, existing orders frozen and payouts held.
- **AC-FR007-04** — Given a REJECTED application, when the vendor resubmits complete documents, then the case re-enters review under the same 48-hour SLA.

## Business Rules Applied

- `BR-VND-01` publishing gate on APPROVED KYC; `BR-VND-02` one store per vendor; `BR-VND-03` 48-hour decision SLA with resubmission.
- `BR-VND-04` suspension effects; `BR-VND-06` Owner-only staff management; `BR-ESC-06` payout eligibility; `BR-PLT-06` audit entries.

## Constraints Honored

- `C-06` vendor identity is phone + OTP — no email-primary or social onboarding.
- `C-17` store operations are configured for domestic (Yemen) fulfillment zones only.

## Dependencies

- **FR:** FR-001 (auth), FR-002 (permissions), FR-004 (publishing gate), FR-008 (store setup), FR-014 (payout gate), FR-017 (notifications), FR-020 (KYC console).
- **NFR/SEC/DEP:** SEC-REQ-011, SEC-REQ-010, NFR-012 (vendor lists product < 10 min once approved), DEP-06, DEP-07.

## Verification Method

- Workflow integration tests: submit → approve → publish; submit → reject → resubmit → approve; suspend mid-order.
- SLA/timer tests: 48-hour escalation fires; publishing gate rejects pre-approval attempts.
- Security tests: malicious file upload rejected; audit rows present for approve/reject/suspend actions.

## Out of Scope Notes

- Legal entity verification beyond document review (no government registry integration in v1).
- Vendors domiciled outside Yemen / cross-border sellers (`C-17`).
- Multiple stores per vendor (`BR-VND-02`), automated AI document approval, paid/subscription vendor plans (`GAP-05`).
