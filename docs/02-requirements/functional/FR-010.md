# FR-010 — Shopping Cart

**Block:** B05 Cart & Checkout · **Priority:** Critical · **Status:** approved · **Registry:** `DOC-REQ-001` §1

FR-010 maintains a multi-vendor cart with hard guards (≤50 distinct products, ≤10 units per product, ≤5 vendors — `C-15`), a 15-minute stock-reservation countdown that starts/refreshes on every add, guest carts kept client-side and merged on login, and server-side total recalculation with price-change re-confirmation. Items that became inactive, out-of-stock or out-of-policy block checkout until removed.

## Rationale

- Traces `OBJ-01` (block B05), `OBJ-12` (`C-13`, `C-14`, `C-15` validation tests) and `OBJ-04` (fast cart on mobile hardware, `NFR-001`).
- Cart guards keep a 5-vendor checkout comprehensible and payable in one wallet transaction — the payoff of `C-10` (master/sub split) and `C-01` (single wallet payment).
- The reservation countdown (`C-13`) is what makes stock truthful before payment; without it the marketplace oversells on hot items.

## Requirements Detail

- Add/update/remove items with immediate localized validation feedback (`BR-PLT-05`, `C-24`).
- Cart guards: ≤50 distinct products, ≤10 units per single product, ≤5 distinct vendors; violations rejected with specific errors (`BR-CRT-01`, `C-15`).
- Adding items starts or refreshes a 15-minute reservation countdown; expiry releases reserved stock (`BR-CRT-02`, `C-13`, FR-005).
- Guest carts live client-side and merge into the account cart on login; on quantity conflict the server value wins (`BR-CRT-03`).
- Totals are always recalculated server-side at checkout; any price change since add-to-cart is displayed for re-confirmation (`BR-CRT-04`).
- Items that are inactive, out-of-stock or out-of-policy block checkout until removed (`BR-CRT-05`).
- Checkout requires wallet balance ≥ order total at confirmation time (`BR-CRT-06`, `C-01`).
- Cart lines are grouped by vendor so the customer can preview the per-vendor split created at order placement (`C-10`).
- Estimated breakdown (items, indicative VAT per `BR-FIN-01`, shipping estimate per `BR-SHP-01`) is shown; the authoritative totals are computed at checkout.
- Server-side cart state is per-user and ownership-scoped (`DATA-REQ-008`); cart reads are cache-friendly for `NFR-001`.
- Guard, price and eligibility checks are re-evaluated server-side at checkout — client-side checks are UX only (`SEC-REQ-004`).

## Preconditions

- Products exist and are ACTIVE with available stock (FR-004/FR-005); the user is browsing (guest or authenticated).
- Reservation infrastructure (Redis/BullMQ, `DEP-03`) is available for the countdown.
- A wallet exists for the balance pre-check at confirmation (FR-013).

## Expected Result

A customer can assemble a compliant multi-vendor cart that holds real stock for 15 minutes, sees accurate server-computed totals and any price changes, and cannot reach an unpayable or policy-violating checkout; expired or ineligible items are surfaced for removal.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **AC-FR010-01** — Given a cart already containing products from 5 vendors, when a 6th vendor's product is added, then the add is rejected with a vendor-limit error.
- **AC-FR010-02** — Given a quantity of 10 for a product, when the customer attempts 11 units, then the update is rejected (≤10 per product).
- **AC-FR010-03** — Given a countdown started at add-to-cart, when 15 minutes elapse without checkout, then reserved stock is released and affected items are flagged stale.
- **AC-FR010-04** — Given a product price changed after add-to-cart, when the customer proceeds to checkout, then the new price is shown and requires re-confirmation.

## Business Rules Applied

- `BR-CRT-01` cart guards (50/10/5); `BR-CRT-02` reservation countdown; `BR-CRT-03` guest merge with server-wins conflict rule.
- `BR-CRT-04` server-side recalculation and price-change re-confirmation; `BR-CRT-05` ineligible items block checkout; `BR-CRT-06` wallet balance ≥ total.

## Constraints Honored

- `C-15` cart guards ≤50 products / ≤10 per product / ≤5 vendors.
- `C-13` 15-minute reservation TTL wired to cart activity.
- `C-14` order value bounds are validated against server-computed totals before checkout completes.
- `C-01` balance gate keeps the cart payable from the wallet only.

## Dependencies

- **FR:** FR-004 (product eligibility), FR-005 (reservation stock), FR-011 (checkout consumes the cart), FR-013 (balance check), FR-019 (coupon preview/eligibility).
- **NFR/DATA/DEP:** NFR-001, NFR-008, DATA-REQ-008, DEP-03 (timers/cache).

## Verification Method

- Unit tests: each guard boundary (50/10/5 at exactly and one over), merge conflict rule, stale-item detection.
- Integration tests: countdown expiry releases stock; guest→login merge; server recalculation after price change.
- E2E: full add-to-cart → checkout hand-off in ar/en RTL, including a blocked-item removal flow.

## Out of Scope Notes

- Persistent wishlists/saved-for-later lists, cart sharing and "move to cart" — not specified in v1.
- Abandoned-cart marketing reminders (marketing automation is out of scope; notifications are transactional).
- Anonymous order placement — checkout requires an authenticated wallet owner (`BR-CRT-06`, `C-01`).
