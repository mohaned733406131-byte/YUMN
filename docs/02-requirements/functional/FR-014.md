# FR-014 — Escrow, Commission & Vendor Payouts

**Block:** B07 Payment & Wallet · **Priority:** Critical · **Status:** approved · **Registry:** `DOC-REQ-001` §1

FR-014 holds buyer funds in escrow for 7 days from DELIVERED before releasing them to vendor payable (blocked by active disputes and return/refund states), computes commission at release as (line subtotal after coupon discount) × per-vendor rate (5–20%, default 10%) with proportional reversal on refunds, batches payouts 3–7 business days after release with a 1,000 YER minimum rollover, draws refunds from escrow first then vendor payable, and reconciles wallet + escrow + payable totals daily.

## Rationale

- Traces `OBJ-02` (escrow releases exactly per `BR-ESC-*`, zero ledger imbalance) and `OBJ-12` (`C-12` escrow hold, `C-01`/`C-04` wallet-only YER settlement).
- The 7-day hold is the buyer-protection window that makes prepaid marketplace trust possible; commission-at-release with reversal keeps platform revenue honest under refunds (`BR-ESC-04`).
- Payout eligibility tied to KYC (`BR-ESC-06`) is the financial counterpart of `FR-007`'s compliance gate.

## Requirements Detail

- On DELIVERED, sub-order funds are held in escrow for 7 days (`BR-ESC-01`, `C-12`).
- Release to vendor payable requires: 7 days elapsed AND no active dispute AND state ∉ {DISPUTED, RETURN_*, REFUNDED} (`BR-ESC-02`).
- Escrow is per sub-order; a dispute freezes only the affected sub-order's escrow while the master completes when all sub-orders settle (`BR-ORD-05`, `BR-ORD-07`, `C-10`).
- Commission = (line subtotal after coupon discount) × commission rate; per-vendor tier 5–20%, default 10% (`BR-ESC-03`).
- Commission is computed at release; later refunds reverse it proportionally (`BR-ESC-04`).
- Payouts are batched and executed 3–7 business days after release; minimum payout 1,000 YER, below-threshold amounts roll over (`BR-ESC-05`).
- Payout requires KYC = APPROVED and a non-suspended store (`BR-ESC-06`, FR-007).
- Refunds draw from escrow-held funds first, then from vendor payable if escrow is insufficient (vendor liability) (`BR-ESC-07`).
- Daily reconciliation: Σ ledger entries balance; wallet + escrow + payable totals equal provider/finance statements; mismatch alerts finance (`BR-ESC-08`, `BR-FIN-03`).
- All escrow movements post balanced double-entry ledger rows; corrections are compensating entries (`BR-PAY-06`, `DATA-REQ-007`).
- Rounding half-up to whole YER per sub-order with differences to the platform rounding account (`BR-FIN-05`).
- Escrow/release jobs run on BullMQ schedules with retries and DLQ (`BR-PLT-01`, `BR-PLT-02`, `C-20`); release checks are idempotent (`BR-PAY-08`).

## Preconditions

- Order payment and escrow funding occurred at placement (FR-011/FR-013); sub-order reached DELIVERED via code verification (FR-015).
- Vendor KYC status and commission tier are known (FR-007, platform settings FR-020).
- No active dispute/return on the sub-order (`BR-ESC-02`).

## Expected Result

Funds move from escrow to vendor payable only after an uneventful 7-day window, commission is taken once (and reversed when refunds occur), payouts are batched under eligibility rules, and every movement is ledgered such that daily reconciliation always balances.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **AC-FR014-01** — Given a sub-order DELIVERED at day 0 with no dispute, when day 7 elapses, then escrow releases to vendor payable automatically exactly once.
- **AC-FR014-02** — Given a sub-order in DISPUTED at day 7, when the release job runs, then no release occurs until admin resolution.
- **AC-FR014-03** — Given a 10,000 YER line subtotal after coupon discount at the default 10% rate, when released, then commission posted is 1,000 YER.
- **AC-FR014-04** — Given a refund after release, when the refund executes, then commission is reversed proportionally and escrow/payable are adjusted per `BR-ESC-07`.

## Business Rules Applied

- `BR-ESC-01` 7-day hold; `BR-ESC-02` release conditions; `BR-ESC-03` commission formula/tier; `BR-ESC-04` proportional reversal.
- `BR-ESC-05` batched payouts 3–7 business days, 1,000 YER minimum rollover; `BR-ESC-06` KYC/suspension gate; `BR-ESC-07` refund drawdown order.
- `BR-ESC-08` daily reconciliation; `BR-ORD-05` dispute freeze; `BR-FIN-05` rounding.

## Constraints Honored

- `C-12` escrow held 7 days from DELIVERED unless a dispute freezes it.
- `C-10` payment/escrow at master funding, settlement and payout per sub-order.
- `C-01`/`C-04` settlement in YER through the wallet ledger only — no cards, crypto or foreign currency.

## Verification Method

- Timer/scheduler tests: release at exactly 7 days, blocked during DISPUTED/RETURN_*/REFUNDED, idempotent re-run.
- Commission math unit tests (tiers 5/10/20, coupon-discounted base, reversal on partial/full refund).
- Reconciliation invariant tests: Σ ledger balance, wallet+escrow+payable == statements, mismatch alerting.

## Out of Scope Notes

- Vendor bank cash-out execution specifics — open item `GAP-06` (automatic vs admin-approved).
- Tiered/subscription commission plans (`GAP-05`), loans/credit to vendors (`C-03`) and multi-currency settlement (`C-04`).
