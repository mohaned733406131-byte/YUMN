# FR-018 — Analytics & Reporting

**Block:** B11 Analytics & Reporting · **Priority:** Medium · **Status:** approved · **Registry:** `DOC-REQ-001` §1

FR-018 delivers role-scoped dashboards and reports: an Admin dashboard (orders, GMV, KYC queue, disputes, delivery ops, platform health), a Vendor dashboard (own-store sales, orders, commission, payouts, refunds, followers), sales/finance/operations report families with tabular export, daily automated financial reconciliation available the next morning with mismatch alerts, and the vendor monthly statement — all in integer YER with locale-aware formatting.

## Rationale

- Traces `OBJ-08` (daily financial reconciliation available next morning; 100% audited admin actions feeding operational reports) and `OBJ-11` (growth metrics tracked from launch — baseline targets pending `ASM-14`).
- Reconciliation reporting is the operational face of `OBJ-02`'s "zero ledger imbalance" measure; without scheduled reports that measure is unobservable.
- Vendor self-service reporting (statement, commission) reduces support load and supports `OBJ-01` block B11.

## Requirements Detail

- Admin dashboard: order/GMV trends, vendor and KYC funnel counts, open disputes, delivery/return SLA indicators, platform health summary.
- Vendor dashboard: own-store sales, order counts, commission withheld, payouts, refunds, follower count — strictly `store_id`-scoped (`BR-VND-07`).
- Report families: sales (orders, units, top products), finance (ledger, wallet, escrow, payable movements), operations (delivery attempts, returns, escalations).
- Daily automated reconciliation across ledger, wallets, escrows, payables and provider statements; mismatch alerts finance (`BR-FIN-03`, `BR-ESC-08`).
- Report for the previous day is available the next morning (`OBJ-08`).
- Vendor monthly statement: sales, commission, refunds, payouts, adjustments (`BR-FIN-04`).
- Tabular export (CSV) of report data for finance workflows; exported totals reconcile to the ledger.
- Report generation runs as background jobs with retries, backoff and DLQ alerting (`BR-PLT-01`, `BR-PLT-02`).
- Amounts rendered as integer YER with `ar-YE` formatting and Arabic-Indic numerals (`BR-PAY-10`, `C-04`); dates locale-aware (`NFR-013`).
- Aggregates/facets cached for read performance (`NFR-004`) within p95 targets (`NFR-001`) at `C-25` load.
- Read scoping per actor: Admin platform scope, Vendor own store, Moderator limited (`BR-ORD-09` analog; `actors-and-roles.md` matrix).

## Preconditions

- Source data exists in orders, ledger, escrow, payout and delivery domains (FR-012–FR-016).
- Scheduler/queue infrastructure (`DEP-03`, BullMQ) is running for daily and monthly jobs.
- Reporting role permissions are defined (FR-002) and the reconciliation baseline (provider statements) is available.

## Expected Result

Operators and vendors can answer "what happened, what is owed, what is wrong" from dashboards and exports that are correct, on schedule, scope-safe and legible in Arabic — with reconciliation discrepancies surfaced automatically rather than discovered manually.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **AC-FR018-01** — Given a vendor requests dashboard data, when processed, then only that store's data is returned and cross-store reads are denied.
- **AC-FR018-02** — Given the daily job runs after midnight, when it completes, then the previous day's reconciliation report is available next morning and mismatches raise alerts.
- **AC-FR018-03** — Given a finance export is generated, when totals are compared to the ledger, then they match exactly (Σ ledger balances).
- **AC-FR018-04** — Given a report job that keeps failing, when 3 retries are exhausted, then it lands in the DLQ and triggers an alert (`BR-PLT-02`).

## Business Rules Applied

- `BR-FIN-03` daily multi-source reconciliation; `BR-FIN-04` vendor monthly statement; `BR-ESC-08` ledger balance and mismatch alerting.
- `BR-PLT-01`/`BR-PLT-02` scheduled jobs, retries and DLQ; `BR-VND-07` store scoping; `BR-PAY-10` integer YER formatting.

## Constraints Honored

- `C-04` single currency — all metrics, exports and statements in YER only.
- `C-25` dashboards and exports remain within `NFR-001` p95 targets at 10,000 concurrent users.

## Dependencies

- **FR:** FR-012 (order facts), FR-013 (wallet/ledger), FR-014 (escrow/commission/payout), FR-020 (admin console embedding), FR-008 (follower counts).
- **NFR/DATA:** NFR-001, NFR-004, NFR-013, NFR-019 (financial retention ≥ 5 years), DATA-REQ-006.
- **DEP:** DEP-02 (PostgreSQL), DEP-03 (queues/cache).

## Verification Method

- Correctness tests against fixture datasets (expected totals precomputed and asserted).
- Scope tests: vendor cross-store denial; moderator/admin read boundaries.
- Scheduler tests: next-morning availability, retry/DLQ path; reconciliation invariant checks; performance test on dashboard endpoints.

## Out of Scope Notes

- Predictive/ML analytics, anomaly-detection models and automated forecasting.
- External BI tool connectors (Power BI/Tableau), data warehouses and streaming real-time analytics.
- Multi-currency or USD-denominated reporting (`C-04`) and marketing attribution/ad-spend reporting (advertising is FUTURE SCOPE).
