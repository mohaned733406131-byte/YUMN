# FR-005 — Inventory Management

**Block:** B02 Product Catalog · **Priority:** Critical · **Status:** approved · **Registry:** `DOC-REQ-001` §1

FR-005 keeps stock truthful under concurrency: stock is an integer ≥0 with oversell prohibited, checkout places a **15-minute TTL reservation**, payment success converts the reservation into a permanent atomic deduction, and cancellation or reservation expiry restores the quantity. All stock movements are idempotent, transactional and reconcilable, with manual vendor adjustments recorded with actor, delta and reason.

## Rationale

- Traces `OBJ-02` (trust-safe payments — oversold orders break buyer trust), `OBJ-12` (`C-13` reservation TTL) and `NFR-008` (idempotency on all stock operations).
- `C-13` exists because Yemeni buyers pre-fund wallets; a lost oversell means a forced refund and a damaged marketplace — the TTL/deduction split is the core of that guarantee.
- Supports `DATA-REQ-006` (write-time validation + reconciliation jobs for stock).

## Requirements Detail

- Stock quantity is an integer ≥ 0 per product/variant; oversell is prohibited (`BR-CAT-07`).
- Checkout creates a reservation with a 15-minute TTL; expiry auto-releases the held quantity (`C-13`, `BR-CRT-02`).
- Payment success performs a permanent, atomic deduction exactly once; reservation → deduction conversion is idempotent (`BR-PLT-03`).
- Cancellation, refund and reservation expiry restore the previously held quantity.
- Concurrent deductions are safe: row-level locking/optimistic concurrency so stock never drops below 0.
- Available stock for display = on-hand − reserved; storefront/search show available (`BR-CAT-06` feeds FR-009).
- Per-variant stock tracking when a product has variants (`BR-CAT-02`).
- Vendor manual stock adjustments (receive/damage/correction) record actor, delta and reason, and are audited (`BR-PLT-06`).
- All stock operations run inside ACID transactions; multi-step order creation uses a saga with compensating stock release (`BR-PLT-04`).
- Reservation timers run as background jobs (BullMQ `{block}.{entity}.{action}` queues, 3 retries + DLQ) (`BR-PLT-01`, `BR-PLT-02`, `C-20`).
- A reconciliation job validates stock vs active reservations and raises mismatches (`DATA-REQ-006`).

## Preconditions

- The product exists and is ACTIVE with stock ≥ 0 (FR-004).
- Redis/BullMQ (`DEP-03`) is running for reservation timers; PostgreSQL (`DEP-02`) enforces constraints.
- A checkout session exists before a reservation can be placed (FR-010/FR-011).

## Expected Result

Customers only ever see and buy real stock: reservations hold quantity for exactly 15 minutes, payment converts them atomically, releases happen automatically on expiry/cancel, and no interleaving of concurrent requests can drive stock negative or double-deduct.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **AC-FR005-01** — Given 3 concurrent checkouts for the last available unit, when all are processed, then exactly one succeeds and stock never goes below 0.
- **AC-FR005-02** — Given a reservation created at t0, when 15 minutes pass without payment, then the reserved quantity is released automatically and available stock rises again.
- **AC-FR005-03** — Given a paid order, when the payment/stock handlers run a second time with the same idempotency key, then stock is deducted exactly once.
- **AC-FR005-04** — Given a paid order that is cancelled, when the cancel flow runs, then the deducted quantity is restored to available stock.

## Business Rules Applied

- `BR-CAT-07` integer stock ≥0, atomic deduction, 15-minute reservation expiry; `BR-CRT-02` countdown start/refresh and release.
- `BR-PLT-03` idempotency keys for stock reservation; `BR-PLT-04` ACID transactions and saga with compensating actions.
- `BR-PLT-01`/`BR-PLT-02` job queue naming, retries and DLQ for expiry timers.

## Constraints Honored

- `C-13` 15-minute TTL hold at checkout, auto-release on expiry, permanent deduction on payment.

## Dependencies

- **FR:** FR-004 (products/variants), FR-010 (cart countdown), FR-011 (checkout reservation), FR-012 (cancel/refund transitions), FR-016 (stock restore on refund).
- **NFR/DATA/DEP:** NFR-008, NFR-010, DATA-REQ-006, DEP-02, DEP-03, `C-20` (BullMQ timers).

## Verification Method

- Concurrency/integration tests: parallel checkout for last unit; double-deduction attempts; reservation release after simulated 15-minute expiry.
- Unit tests: available = on-hand − reserved math, adjustment delta validation, saga compensation paths.
- Reconciliation test: stock vs reservations report surfaces mismatches (`DATA-REQ-006`); constraint test for `C-13` TTL.

## Out of Scope Notes

- Multi-warehouse/bin-level inventory, transfers and per-location stock — single logical stock pool per product in v1.
- Backorders, negative stock, pre-orders and low-stock auto-reorder/forecasting — explicitly not supported (oversell prohibited).
- Cross-border or multi-tenant stock sharing (`C-17` domestic fulfillment only).
