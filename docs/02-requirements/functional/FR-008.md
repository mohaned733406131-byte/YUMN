# FR-008 — Store Management & Storefront Configuration

**Block:** B03 Store Management · **Priority:** High · **Status:** approved · **Registry:** `DOC-REQ-001` §1

FR-008 gives each vendor one configurable storefront: profile and bilingual branding assets, domestic shipping zone configuration, operating settings (including auto-accept of new orders), merchant return-policy defaults, and the follow-store feature (customers follow; vendors see follower list/count; followers receive new-product and offer notifications). All store data is strictly scoped to the owning vendor and hidden when the store is suspended.

## Rationale

- Traces `OBJ-01` (block B03) and `OBJ-03` (Arabic-first bilingual storefront quality); also supports `OBJ-11` (liquidity) through the follower mechanism.
- Zone and return-policy configuration are where `C-17` (domestic fulfillment) and `C-11` (merchant-configurable returns) become operable rather than declarative.
- One store per vendor (`BR-VND-02`) keeps commission, KYC, fulfillment and settlement unambiguous for `FR-014`.

## Requirements Detail

- Store profile: bilingual (ar/en) names and description, logo/banner branding assets, contact details, with upload security (`SEC-REQ-011`, `C-24`).
- Exactly one store per vendor account in v1 (`BR-VND-02`).
- Shipping zone configuration per store, restricted to domestic Yemen coverage; feeds shipping-fee calculation (`BR-SHP-01`, `C-17`).
- Operating settings: order auto-accept toggle (System may auto-confirm PLACED → CONFIRMED per the transition guard) and other store-level operational switches.
- Return-policy defaults: `isReturnable`, `returnPeriodDays` applied to the store's products (`C-11`, consumed by `BR-RET-01`).
- Read-only visibility of the store's commission tier (5–20%, default 10%) as configured by the platform (`BR-ESC-03`).
- Follow-store: customers follow/unfollow; vendors see follower list and count; followers get new-product/offer notifications (`BR-VND-05`, FR-017).
- Suspended store: products hidden from storefront, new orders blocked, existing orders frozen, payouts held (`BR-VND-04`).
- Only ACTIVE products are rendered on the storefront (`BR-CAT-06`).
- Every vendor-facing query and mutation is scoped to `store_id` (`BR-VND-07`, `DATA-REQ-008`).
- Staff (Viewer/Editor/Manager) operate the store per role; only the Owner changes staff roles (`BR-VND-06`).
- Storefront displays the aggregated store rating from visible reviews (`BR-REV-05`).

## Preconditions

- Vendor account exists and is authenticated (FR-001/FR-007); store created (one per vendor).
- Domestic zone list and rating data sources exist (FR-015 zones; FR-006 ratings).
- Storage (`DEP-07`) accepts branding assets within upload limits.

## Expected Result

Vendors fully configure a compliant, bilingual storefront that renders only active products, operates within domestic zones and their return policy, and — when suspended — disappears from customer-facing discovery while freezing new commercial activity.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **AC-FR008-01** — Given a vendor who already owns a store, when a second store creation is submitted, then it is rejected (one store per vendor).
- **AC-FR008-02** — Given a customer follows a store, when the store publishes a new product, then followers receive a notification subject to their preferences.
- **AC-FR008-03** — Given a suspended store, when its storefront is requested, then its products are hidden and new orders are blocked.
- **AC-FR008-04** — Given a shipping zone outside domestic coverage, when saved, then the configuration is rejected (`C-17`).

## Business Rules Applied

- `BR-VND-02` one store per vendor; `BR-VND-04` suspension effects; `BR-VND-05` follower feature; `BR-VND-06` staff roles; `BR-VND-07` `store_id` scoping.
- `BR-CAT-06` ACTIVE-only storefront visibility; `BR-REV-05` aggregated store rating; `BR-SHP-01` zone-based shipping fee input.

## Constraints Honored

- `C-11` merchant-configurable return policy per store/product.
- `C-17` shipping zones limited to domestic Yemen fulfillment.
- `C-24` bilingual (ar default / en) storefront and settings text.

## Dependencies

- **FR:** FR-004 (catalog), FR-007 (KYC/onboarding), FR-006 (ratings), FR-015 (zones/fees), FR-017 (follower notifications), FR-019 (banners), FR-020 (oversight/suspension).
- **NFR/DATA:** NFR-013, DATA-REQ-008, `SEC-REQ-011` (asset uploads).

## Verification Method

- Integration tests: one-store constraint, zone validation, return-policy persistence, suspension visibility rules.
- Unit tests: follower list/count aggregation, storefront product filtering (ACTIVE only).
- E2E: bilingual storefront rendering (RTL ar / LTR en), follow → notification fan-out.

## Out of Scope Notes

- Multiple storefronts per vendor (`BR-VND-02`), custom domains, vendor-supplied theme code and template marketplaces.
- Advanced operating configurations beyond the canon (delivery scheduling windows, staff permission customization).
- Cross-border zones (`C-17`) and offline/pop-up store concepts.
